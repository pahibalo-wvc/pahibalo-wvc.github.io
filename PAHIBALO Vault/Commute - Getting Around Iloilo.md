# Getting Around Iloilo

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Senior advice for getting from campus to the places dormers go most, like SM City, Festive Walk, Jaro Plaza and the La Paz Public Market.

## Starting points (to confirm)
- Campus is in Brgy. Bito-on, Jaro, Iloilo City.
- Iloilo renumbered and color-coded its jeepney routes (2024), so older guides may be wrong.
- Fares: the national minimum jeepney fare rose on 19 March 2026; Iloilo's exact fare (unknown). Every fare on this page will say "approximate, checked [date]".

## Places dormers go
These are the places seniors say dormers go to most from campus. Routes to each one are still being collected.
- SM City Iloilo
- Festive Walk
- Jaro Plaza
- Leganes
- Atria
- Ungka
- La Paz Public Market
- Western Visayas Medical Center
- Downtown Iloilo City stores, for DIY and project supplies
- University of San Agustin, on General Luna Street
- West Visayas State University, in La Paz
