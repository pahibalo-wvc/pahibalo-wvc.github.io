# Biology

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Biology contests for WVC scholars, led by the Philippine Biology Olympiad. You get in through the biology pool, by passing its selection exam or by a teacher's recommendation.

## What it is
WVC sends scholars to biology contests from its biology pool, a group of students who train together for competitions. The biggest one is the [[Comp - Philippine Biology Olympiad]] (PBO), which picks the four students who represent the country at the International Biology Olympiad.

## How to get in
STEM contests at WVC go through subject pools. To join the biology pool, either pass the pool's selection exam or get a recommendation from your biology teacher. Pool members are the ones sent to biology contests.
