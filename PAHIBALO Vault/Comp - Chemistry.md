## What it is
WVC sends scholars to chemistry contests from its chemistry pool, a group of students who train together for competitions.

The main national contest is the Philippine National Chemistry Olympiad (PNCO), run by the Philippine Federation of Chemistry Societies. Its national finals have three parts: a written exam, a laboratory practical exam and a chemistry quiz bee. The top finishers form the Philippine team of up to four students for the International Chemistry Olympiad (IChO). In 2025 the 24th PNCO finals were held in March at UP Manila, and the four gold medalists, all from the PSHS System, went on to the 57th IChO in Dubai.

The PNCO starts with regional rounds. At the 2022 regional round, PSHS-WVC took the top three spots.

## How to get in
STEM contests at WVC go through subject pools. To join the chemistry pool, either pass the pool's selection exam or get a recommendation from your chemistry teacher. Pool members are the ones sent to chemistry contests.
