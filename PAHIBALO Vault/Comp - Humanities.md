# Humanities

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Journalism and Model United Nations contests. For journalism, you get in through a teacher's recommendation, by joining Banaag or Sci-Link, or by passing a screening.

## What it is
The humanities contests at WVC are campus journalism and debate. Journalists write and broadcast for the [[Comp - Schools Press Conference]], from the division level up. Delegates speak and negotiate at [[Comp - Model United Nations]] conferences.

## How to get in
For journalism, there are three ways in:
- A teacher recommends you.
- You join a school paper, [[Org - Banaag]] (Filipino) or [[Org - Sci-Link]] (English).
- You pass the screening for the press conference team.

For MUN, delegates come from [[Org - MUN Org]].

## Competitions
- [[Comp - Schools Press Conference]]: DepEd's campus journalism contests, from division to regional and national.
- [[Comp - Model United Nations]]: UN committee simulations, hosted at WVC and other schools.

## Skills it tests
- News, feature, editorial, science and sports writing, in English and Filipino
- Broadcasting and page layout, in the group contests
- Public speaking, research and negotiation, at MUN
