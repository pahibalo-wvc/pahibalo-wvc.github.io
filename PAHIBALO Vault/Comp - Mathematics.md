# Mathematics

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Math and statistics contests for WVC scholars, led by the Philippine Mathematical Olympiad. You get in through the math or statistics pool, by passing its selection exam or by a teacher's recommendation.

## What it is
WVC sends scholars to math contests from its math pool, and to statistics contests from its statistics pool. Each pool is a group of students who train together for competitions. The biggest math contest is the [[Comp - Philippine Mathematical Olympiad]] (PMO).

## How to get in
STEM contests at WVC go through subject pools. To join the math pool or the statistics pool, either pass the pool's selection exam or get a recommendation from your math or statistics teacher. Pool members are the ones sent to contests.

## Competitions
- [[Comp - Philippine Mathematical Olympiad]]: the national math olympiad, with qualifying, area and national stages.

- Regional Statistics Quiz Bee: a statistics contest for Western Visayas schools. PSHS-WVC has won it six years in a row.

## Skills it tests
- Solving problems quickly and accurately in timed written exams
- Writing complete mathematical proofs
- Checking your work, since many answers must be exact
