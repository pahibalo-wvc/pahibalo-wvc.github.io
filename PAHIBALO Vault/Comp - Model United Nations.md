# Model United Nations

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Debate simulations of UN committees.

## What it is
A Model United Nations (MUN) is a simulation of United Nations committees. Each student acts as a delegate for one country, debates the committee's topic and works with other delegates on a resolution, following a set of rules of procedure.

PSHS-WVC hosted its first PISAY-MUN on 6 to 7 February 2026, with three committees: UNSC, UN-Habitat and UNHRC. The UNSC committee discussed counter-terrorism strategies. West Visayas MUN 2026 ran at WVSU from 19 to 21 March 2026. PisayMUN-WVC won Best Delegation at the 8th San Agustin Model United Nations (SAMUN), October 14 to 16, 2025, for the second year in a row. At the PSHS Main Campus MUN in 2024, each delegation wrote and submitted a position paper before the conference.

## Skills it tests
- Public speaking and debate
- Researching a country's position and writing a position paper
- Negotiating with other delegates to write a resolution
- Following formal rules of procedure
