# Philippine Biology Olympiad

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> National biology olympiad that picks the Philippine IBO team.

## What it is
The Philippine Biology Olympiad (PBO) is the main national biology contest for Grades 7 to 12. The Department of Biology of Ateneo de Manila University hosts it. It picks the four students who represent the Philippines at the International Biology Olympiad (IBO).

Every round has a theory exam and a practical exam. The theory tests follow the IBO style, with true-or-false items. In the elimination round the practical test is taken online, while the semifinal and final practicals are done in a laboratory. In the 2023 edition, the top 55 moved on to the semifinals, the top 20 to the finals, and the top 4 became the Philippine team. Semifinalists also went through a month of lectures and workshops.

## Skills it tests
- Biology knowledge across many topics, in the theory exam
- Laboratory skills, in the practical exam
- Scientific reasoning, since each item asks you to judge statements about biology as true or false
