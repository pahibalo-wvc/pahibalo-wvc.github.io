# Philippine Mathematical Olympiad

Back to [[Home]]

> National math olympiad for high school students.

## What it is
The Philippine Mathematical Olympiad (PMO) is a national math contest for high school students. The Mathematical Society of the Philippines runs it with DOST-SEI. It has three stages: a written qualifying stage, an area stage, and a national stage for the top 20 of the area stage.

In the 26th PMO papers, the qualifying stage had two multiple-choice parts and a third part where every answer is a whole number from 1 to 999. The area stage had a short-answer part and a second part where you write full proofs on solution sheets, worth ten points each. The DepEd memo for the 27th PMO describes the qualifying stage as a three-hour contest. The 28th PMO had its qualifying stage on 18 October 2025 and its area stage on 13 December 2025. Dates for the next edition are (unknown).

## Skills it tests
- Solving math problems quickly and accurately in a timed written exam
- Writing complete mathematical proofs, in the area stage
- Checking your work, since each short answer must be an exact whole number
