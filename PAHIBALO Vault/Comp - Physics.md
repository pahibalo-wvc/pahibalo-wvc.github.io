# Physics

> [!summary] In short
> Physics contests for WVC scholars, led by the Philippine Physics Olympiad. You get in through the physics pool, by passing its selection exam or by a teacher's recommendation.

## What it is
WVC sends scholars to physics contests from its physics pool, a group of students who train together for competitions.

The main national contest is the Philippine Physics Olympiad (PPO), run by the Samahang Pisika ng Pilipinas. It looks for students who are strong in physics and trains them for international contests. Its national winners are invited to represent the country at the International Physics Olympiad (IPhO). In 2022, the five national winners formed the IPhO team and won a bronze medal and an honorable mention.

## How to get in
STEM contests at WVC go through subject pools. To join the physics pool, either pass the pool's selection exam or get a recommendation from your physics teacher. Pool members are the ones sent to physics contests.
