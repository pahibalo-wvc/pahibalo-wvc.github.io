## What it is
PubliSCIENCE is not a student org. It is the research publication site presented by the [[Office - Research Unit]], at publiscience.org. It has event reports, the Publiscience Journal (Volumes 1 to 6), and other publications like PAMAAGI, a COVID-19 storybook, Project Talampad and Tanarau.

It sits with competitions because getting your research into the journal is something seniors work toward, next to the [[Event - Research Events]]. How papers get picked for the journal is (unknown).

## Skills it tests
- Doing a full research study, from method to results
- Scientific writing for a journal
