# Schools Press Conference

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> DepEd campus journalism contests: Division (DSPC) and Regional (RSPC).

## What it is
The Schools Press Conference is the yearly campus journalism contest of DepEd. Schools compete first at the Division Schools Press Conference (DSPC), then at the Regional Schools Press Conference (RSPC) for Western Visayas. For 2026, only first-place RSPC winners went on to the National Schools Press Conference (NSPC). RSPC 2026 (secondary) was in Iloilo City, 13 to 14 February 2026.

Individual contests are held in English and in Filipino. They include news writing, feature writing, editorial writing, science and technology writing and sports writing. Group contests include radio scriptwriting and broadcasting and collaborative desktop publishing, with teams of seven (five for online publishing). Division conferences have also held photojournalism and captioning contests. The exact list changes each year, so check that year's DepEd memo.

## Skills it tests
- News, feature, editorial, science and sports writing
- Writing in both English and Filipino
- Radio scriptwriting and broadcasting, in the group contest
- Page layout and desktop publishing
- Photojournalism and writing captions
- Working as a team on a deadline, in the group contests

## How WVC joins
You get on the team in one of three ways: a teacher recommends you, you join a school paper ([[Org - Banaag]] or [[Org - Sci-Link]]), or you pass the screening. See [[Comp - Humanities]].
