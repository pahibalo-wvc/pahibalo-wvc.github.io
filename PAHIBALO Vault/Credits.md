# Credits

Back to [[Home]]

The mascots and the PAHIBALO logo were drawn by Laniog D. The site is built and kept up by the PAHIBALO org at PSHS-WVC.

## Sources
Most facts come from the PSHS-WVC Student Handbook 2018 and the 2025 PSHS Student Code of Conduct and Interventions. Others come from the old Discipline Office site, the PSHS-WVC info systems portal, org primers, blank school forms, and public posts on the PSHS-WVC, Banaag and Batch Astra Facebook pages. Each page lists its own source at the bottom.

The GWA Calculator was inspired by calc.dantis.me, a calculator made by another student. No code was copied from it.

The PSHS logo is used with permission. In the header and footer it sits behind the PAHIBALO logo, like two coins held up together, to show the site is made by PSHS-WVC students. On the home page, the logo carries a small Pisay pin added by the PAHIBALO team; the mascot art itself is unchanged.
