> [!summary] In short
> Daily schedules, curfew, laundry, kitchen and visitor rules for dormers, from the current GRH notices and the 2018 handbook.

## Daily schedule
These times are from the notice board of the girls' residence hall (GRH). The boys' residence hall (BRH) may post slightly different times.

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Wake-up time | 6:00 to 6:30 AM, Monday to Friday |
| Out of the residence hall | 7:30 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday to Thursday, and 7:30 AM to 2:00 PM on Friday. Interns are not allowed inside during these hours. |
| Dorm and campus curfew | 7:00 PM, every day |
| Study period | 8:00 to 9:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday |
| Ironing | 6:00 to 9:00 AM and 6:00 to 9:00 PM on Saturday and Sunday; 5:00 to 7:00 PM on Wednesday |

## Laundry
Interns may wash only light garments themselves, like shorts, underwear, socks and handkerchiefs. Everything else goes to your laundry woman with a [[Form - Laundry Slip]].

- Do your laundry before study period.
- The washing machine is off limits. Only the spin dryer may be used.
- Unplug the spin dryer after use, and turn off the lights and ceiling fan before you leave.
- Keep the laundry room clean and throw soap and fabric conditioner wrappers in the trash can.

The laundry room is at the back of the GRH Annex. It is open from 5:00 to 6:30 PM on Monday and Wednesday, and from 4:30 to 6:00 PM on Friday.

The spin dryer (and washing, per a handwritten note on the schedule) runs from 5:00 to 7:30 PM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and from 6:00 to 9:00 AM on Saturday and Sunday. The notice also lists a 6:00 to 8:00 PM slot without saying which days.

## Kitchen
- The kitchen is open on weekends, from 6:00 to 8:30 AM and from 6:00 to 9:30 PM, or during Residence Hall activities with the Residence Hall staff.
- To use it for an ALA or class activity, get permission from the Residence Hall Head. Your subject teacher has to supervise.
- Do not take kitchenware, utensils or other things out of the kitchen without permission.
- Unplug all appliances and turn off the gas valve after use, and keep the kitchen clean.

| Appliance | When you can use it |
|---|---|
| Refrigerator | 6:00 to 9:00 AM and 6:00 to 10:00 PM, Monday to Friday; 6:00 to 8:30 AM and 6:00 to 9:30 PM, Saturday and Sunday |
| Microwave and toaster | 6:00 to 8:30 AM and 6:00 to 9:30 PM |

## Visitors
All visitors ask permission from the Residence Hall Head or staff and sign the Residence Hall visitors' logbook. They wait at the first-floor lobby or on the covered walk in front of the residence hall.

The 2018 handbook gave visiting hours of 7:00 AM to 12 noon on Saturday and Sunday, and 3:00 to 8:00 PM on holidays. Whether these still apply is (unknown).

## Emergencies
Police, fire, rescue and hospital numbers for Iloilo City are on [[Dorm - Emergency Numbers]]. The numbers of the GRH dorm manager and assistant dorm managers are posted on the notice board.

## Who gets a slot
Priority goes to Grade 7 and 8 students from the provinces on full scholarship (2018).

## Fees
Lodging was ₱60 a month plus electricity, taken from your stipend (2018). Bring your own bedding.

## Leaving campus
File a [[Form - Leave Pass]]. More rules are in the Dormitory Handbook given to residents.
