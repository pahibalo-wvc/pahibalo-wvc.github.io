# Acquaintance Party

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> A party in the first week of the school year where scholars from every grade meet and get to know their schoolmates.

## When
In 2026 it was on Friday, July 24, the same week as the opening program (July 20) and the [[Event - Toast to Excellence]] (July 23). Students call it Akwe. It comes right after Fun Day, the day of games between teams, so the two are often posted together as "Fun Day and Akwe".

## What happens
The [[Org - Student Alliance]] runs the party on an outdoor stage, decorated for a theme. Past themes include the 2000s ("Pisay2k") and a 2016-era "Party Campus". Students sit on the ground in front of the stage and watch dance numbers, band sets and other performances, and the SA posts the program flow beforehand. It is the first big chance to meet schoolmates outside your own batch.

Dormers also have a separate Dorm Acquaintance Party in the residence halls.

%% Themes from Student Alliance posts ("Pisay2k: Acquaintance Party Program Flow" and "the Party Campus is back... the 2016 era"); their years are not shown on the posts. Dorm Acquaintance Party 2024: Sci-Link post. %%

## What to prepare
Check the Student Alliance page for the theme, since many students dress up for it. Fun Day comes first, so bring a change of clothes if you join the games.
