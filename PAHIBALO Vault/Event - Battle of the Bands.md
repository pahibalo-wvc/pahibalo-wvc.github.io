# Battle of the Bands

Back to [[Home]]

## When
Around the third quarter. The exact date is (unknown).

## What happens
Student bands play on stage and compete against each other. A big crowd usually comes to watch, and food stalls set up around the venue. By the end of the night it feels like a party.

The [[Org - Student Alliance]] runs it and gives it a name each year. In 2025 it was called War of the Waves, with seven bands competing, and the SA revealed the bands on its Facebook page before the night.

Bands also played at Ugnayan 2025 (June 12, 2025, in the New Auditorium), where a battle of the bands closed a day of sports events.
