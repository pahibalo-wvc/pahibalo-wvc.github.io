# Buwan ng Wika at Kasaysayan

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The school's language and history month program, held in the New Auditorium in August. Expect plays, traditional dances, songs and talks on Philippine history.

## What happens
The school celebrates Buwan ng Wika and Buwan ng Kasaysayan together. Expect plays, traditional dances, singing and other performances, along with talks on Philippine history. In 2026 the program was on August 22 in the New Auditorium, one day later than planned because classes were cancelled.

The 2026 theme was about the Filipino language and AI as tools for research. A guest speaker from West Visayas State University talked about using AI properly in language research.
