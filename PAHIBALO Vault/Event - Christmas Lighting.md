# Christmas Lighting

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The whole school lights the campus Christmas tree. Classes parade parols made from recyclables, batches compete in caroling, and a school-wide raffle gives away big prizes.

## What happens
The Christmas Lighting starts the Christmas season on campus. In 2025 it was held on the afternoon of November 28.

Each class makes its own banner and parol out of recyclable materials. Most students wear red for the day. The classes then march them around the oval in a parade contest, led by the West Visayas State University band. After the parade, there is a community dance and a caroling contest between batches, with categories and special awards like "Best in Improvised Instruments". The program ends with a raffle (bunot-bunot), and it is the part most students wait for. Names are drawn from the whole school, and there are many big prizes.

## The 2026 program
The Student Alliance posted the 2026 Pisay Christmas Lantern Lighting program. It ran from 3:00 PM to 7:30 PM: the parade lineup at 3:00 PM, the Christmas Parade from 3:25 PM, the opening program, Paint Me a Picture, two Paskwa raffle draws run by the PTA, Christmas caroling by the students, then the Grand Christmas Lantern and Tree Lighting with a countdown from 6:45 PM. The program closed with remarks and photos at 7:15 PM.

The contests in 2026 were Paint Me a Picture, a GAD-ified Parol-Making Competition, a Human Christmas Tree Showcase and Christmas Caroling. For the parol contest, each section got a 1 m by 1 m bare parol to decorate on the theme "Paghili-ugyon". At least 70 percent of it had to be recycled or upcycled materials, it had to light up from inside (LED lights recommended), and a section could spend at most PHP 1,000. One representative from each section presented the parol for 2 minutes during the lighting.

## What to prepare
Your class would usually start planning its banner, parol and caroling piece a few weeks ahead. Collect recyclable materials early, since the banner and parol have to be made from them.

If the program keeps you on campus after class hours, ask your adviser whether you need a [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF). Activities outside class hours usually need one.
