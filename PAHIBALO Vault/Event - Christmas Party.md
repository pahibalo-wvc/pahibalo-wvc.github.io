## What happens
Each section holds its own Christmas party, and dormers have a separate Residence Hall Christmas Party. Parties usually have a gift exchange, icebreaker games and a lot of food. Many classes also take time to share personal stories, so it can get emotional.

The school has also held a campus-wide year-end party called Paskwa sa Pisay, an indoor party with a stage, lights and music, which Sci-Link described as a year-end acquaintance party. At the Dorm Christmas Party, dormers exchange gifts and perform songs, and interns have joined in too.
