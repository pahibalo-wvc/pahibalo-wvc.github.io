# Dormers Day

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> A day for dormers in late November. Dormers cook in groups, play games at the gym, and spend the evening together eating at the Coop.

## When
In 2026 it was on Sunday, November 22. In 2025 it was on November 11. Dormers also have their own Christmas party in December (see [[Event - Christmas Party]]).

%% Date from the SAU Calendar Tracker SY 2026-2027, November tab ("Dormers' Day 2026"). Whether the [[Office - Residence Halls Unit]] runs it is (unknown). %%

## What happens
Dormers cook food in groups and play games at the gym. In the evening they stay together and eat at the [[Office - Cooperative]] until night. In 2025 the games included balloon relays and chair races in a hall decorated with balloons and a "Happy Dormers' Day" backdrop, with the rest of the dormers crowding around to cheer.
