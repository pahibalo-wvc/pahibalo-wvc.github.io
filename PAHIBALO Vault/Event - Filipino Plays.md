# Filipino Plays

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Grades 8 to 10 stage plays based on classic Filipino works like Noli Me Tangere and Ibong Adarna. Classes prepare for months and handle the acting, script, props and technical work.

## What happens
Each class stages a play based on a classic Filipino work, such as Noli Me Tangere, El Filibusterismo, Florante at Laura or Ibong Adarna. Preparation takes months. The play tests your acting, scriptwriting, props and technical work. Some plays are shown to an audience and some are not.

Batch 2027 held an El Filibusterismo Play Fest on May 15, 2024, when it was in Grade 9. Each group gave its version its own title, and staged it in the auditorium with its own set and posters. Batches also stage an English play.
