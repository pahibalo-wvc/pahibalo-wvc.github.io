# Income Generating Projects

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Classes and batches sometimes run small one-day businesses, like food stalls around campus, and the rest of the school buys from them.

## What happens
An income generating project (IGP) is a small business run by a class or batch for a day or a few days, usually food. The stalls set up at the octagons near the oval and the girls' dorm, at the backstage, or in front of the [[Office - Cooperative]].

In Grade 11, one IGP is graded: classes run food stalls as an alternative assessment in Social Science (economics). In 2025 it ran on two afternoons in November, with two classes selling each day.

If your class is running one, plan your products, prices and stall schedule weeks ahead.
