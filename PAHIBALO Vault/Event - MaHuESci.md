# MaHuESci

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> A school-wide celebration in November with a quiz bee, festival costumes for each batch and a bench cheer competition.

## What happens
MaHuESci 2026 opened on November 11. What the name stands for is (unknown).

On Day 2 of MaHuESci 2026, each batch dressed as a festival from Western Visayas: Batch 2027 as the MassKara Festival (Negros Occidental), Batch 2028 as the Binirayan Festival (Antique), Batch 2029 as the Ati-Atihan Festival (Aklan), Batch 2030 as the Dinagyang Festival (Iloilo), Batch 2031 as the Manggahan Festival (Guimaras) and Batch 2032 as the Halaran Festival (Capiz). A bench cheer competition between the batches followed.

Day 1 started with a Mass and worship service, then a guest speaker talked about science, math and technology. Teams from different grades competed in the MaHuESci Quiz Bee. In the afternoon, the Grade 12 research poster presentation was held for Grades 10 to 12 ([[Event - Research Events]]).
