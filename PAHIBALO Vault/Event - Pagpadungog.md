# Pagpadungog

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The school's recognition program at the end of the school year, where Director's List scholars and other awardees receive their awards.

## What happens
Pagpadungog is the recognition program held near the end of the school year. In 2026 it was on May 20 at 1:00 PM in the New Auditorium, with a physics professor from UP Los Baños as the recognition speaker.

Which awards are given is explained in [[Guide - Honors and Awards]].
