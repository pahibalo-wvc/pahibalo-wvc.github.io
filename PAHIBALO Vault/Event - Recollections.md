# Recollections

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Retreats held by section and by batch, usually at a resort. Expect games and sports, time to relax, and honest sharing with classmates you may not know well yet.

## When
During long breaks or the summer. Recollections start in Grade 9.

## What happens
A recollection is a retreat for one section or a whole batch. It is usually held at a resort, with games, sports and free time to relax. A big part of it is sharing personal stories, so it can get emotional, and many scholars make new friends there.

A batch recollection can also be held on campus. Batch 2027 held its recollection in the auditorium in May 2024, with talks on stage and group games among the seats. Batch committees such as creatives, ambassadors and publicity ran it, and each got a certificate of appreciation.

## What to prepare
Your section or batch sets everything up, from the venue to the program. If the recollection is off campus, you will usually need a signed [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF).
