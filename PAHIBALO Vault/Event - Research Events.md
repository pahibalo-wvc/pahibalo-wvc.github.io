# Research Events (PAGs)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The research events a Grade 12 batch has to finish to graduate, like the research poster presentation. Grades 10 and 11 come to listen and help evaluate.

## What they are
The PAGs are the research events that a batch needs to finish before graduation. Each one has its own name, and the names change from batch to batch, so ask your batch officers for this year's list. The batch runs each event with committees made up of its own members.

## The poster presentation
In one of the PAGs, Grade 12 scholars present their research as posters, so it is mostly practice in presenting research. Grade 10 and 11 scholars come as the audience and also help evaluate the presenters. Between presentations, there are parlor games like bingo and hulog piso. In 2026 it was held in the New Auditorium in November, in the afternoon.

## How the batch runs it
The batch picks only a few members for each committee. Joining a committee counts as one event on the batch's participation tracker.

The Creatives and Technicals committee makes the program slides, certificates and poster layouts, and takes photos. Programs and Publicity handles the program flow, floor plans, teasers and articles. Registration and Participant Services runs registration, attendance, loyalty cards, snacks and the evaluation form. Logistics and Operations buys the materials and handles the poster stands, printing and money records.
