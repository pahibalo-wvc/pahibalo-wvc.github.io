# Sinadya Party

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The Student Alliance's party after the exams, held on the open lawn by a decorated stage. Expect live performances, lawn games and dancing until the night.

## When
Right after the exams. The Student Alliance announces it with a post along the lines of "Exams done, guess what is next", so it works as the reward for finishing the quarter. Sinadya 2025 was the third one (Sinadya 3.0), and the newly elected Student Alliance officers for SY 2025-2026 took their oath during it, so it falls near the end of the school year.

%% "Near the end of the school year" is inferred from the oath taking of the next year's SA officers at Sinadya 3.0. The exact month is (unknown). %%

## What happens
The [[Org - Student Alliance]] sets up an open-air stage on the lawn, decorated with balloons and banners. Students watch live performances from bands and other acts, play group games on the grass, and dance once it gets dark. In 2025 the party also included the oath taking of the new Student Alliance officers.

## What to prepare
Come in comfortable clothes, since the games are on the grass. If the party keeps you on campus after class hours, ask your adviser whether you need a [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF).
