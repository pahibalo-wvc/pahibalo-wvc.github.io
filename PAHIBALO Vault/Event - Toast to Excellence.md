# Toast to Excellence

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The ceremony that moves a batch into the Specialization Years Program (SYP). The center of it is a pinning ceremony.

## What happens
Toast to Excellence marks the start of a batch's SYP years. In 2026, Batch 2028 had theirs on July 23 in the New Auditorium.

In 2026, the Campus Director opened the program with a message to the batch, and an alumnus from Batch 2022 gave the guest talk. The main part is the pinning ceremony.

PSHS splits its six years into three programs. The Foundation Years Program (FYP) and the Advancement Years Program (AYP) come first, and the SYP is last.
