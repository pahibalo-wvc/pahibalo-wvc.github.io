# Yearbook Creation and Launch

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The Grade 12 batch makes its own yearbook, with creative shots of every batchmate and the story of its years together. The shoot is during class time, so you need a signed parent consent form.

## What happens
The Grade 12 batch puts together its own yearbook. It has creative shots of every batchmate, the history of the batch and the experiences they shared.

Grade 12 scholars have their creative photos taken for the batch yearbook. For Batch 2027 it was in the New Auditorium in October 2026. A teacher-adviser goes with you, and you get time to make up any requirements you miss.

## What you need
A signed [[Form - Parent Consent Form]], submitted to the organizer or teacher-adviser.
