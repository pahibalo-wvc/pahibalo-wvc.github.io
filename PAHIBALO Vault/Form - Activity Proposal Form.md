# Activity Proposal Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The proposal an org files to get an activity approved. Needed before you can book a venue or spend school funds.

## Who needs it and when
Org officers planning an activity. The handbook says all org and ALA activities need approval before they happen. One APF can cover a whole year's set of events: the SDC filed one APF for Afterburn, Intramurals, the Fun Run and YUPISAY.

## Steps
1. Fill in the title of the activity, the rationale (why you are holding it) and a brief description of each event.
2. Add the venue, the duration and schedule (you can attach the schedule), and the intended participants with how many are male and female.
3. Write the target accomplishment, which is what a successful activity would look like.
4. Fill in the line-item budget with the items, quantity, source of funds (PSHS or other sources), total and remarks. If you will collect money from students, say so here. If there is no budget, write "N/A".
5. List any other school resources you need.
6. Sign as "Prepared by" (org president) and get your adviser's "Noted by".
7. Route it for the remaining signatures, in order.
8. Once approved, it gets an AP number in the format EC/CC-YYYY-NNN-VVV.
9. If you need a venue or equipment, file the [[Form - FAD Form]] next.

## How it gets approved
Hand it in at least 10 days before the activity. Submit it to the SAU Chief, the head of the [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]], on paper or by email.

1. The SAU Chief checks it, gives it an APF number and an initial signature, then sends it to the CID Chief.
2. The CID Chief checks it. If there is no budget request, it goes straight to the Campus Director.
3. If there is a budget request, it goes to the Budget Officer, then the Accountant, then the FAD Chief, and then the Campus Director.
4. The Campus Director decides and sends it back to you through the Campus Director's secretary.

For an activity off campus or with real safety risks, the SAU also has a [[Form - Project and Risk Management Plan]] you can attach. Within 7 days after the activity, file an [[Form - Activity Report Form]] (ARF). Joining a competition or training only counts as official once the Campus Director approves it through an APF. Student activities are noted by the academic unit head, coordinators or advisers, or by the SSD Chief for activities the SSD starts.

## Who signs
1. Org president (prepared by)
2. Org adviser (noted by)
3. DSA Chief or CID Chief (recommending approval). For an activity started by the Office of the Executive Director (OED), the RPAD Chief signs instead.
4. Accountant or budget officer, budget officer, and FAD Chief (certificate that funds are available)
5. Campus Director (approves). For an OED activity, the Executive Director approves.

## Where to submit
The SAU Chief at the [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]], at least 10 days before the activity.
