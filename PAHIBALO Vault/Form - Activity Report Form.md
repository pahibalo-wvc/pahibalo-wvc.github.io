# Activity Report Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The report an org files after an approved activity: what happened, what it achieved and what to do better next time.

## Who needs it and when
The organizer of any activity approved through an [[Form - Activity Proposal Form]] (APF). File it within 7 days after the activity. An org also needs its accomplishment report from last year to get reaccredited (see [[Form - Org Accreditation Checklist]]).

## Steps
1. Write the AR number. It follows your APF number, in the format EC/CC-YYYY-NNN.
2. Write the title and date of the activity.
3. Write what happened during the activity.
4. Write the specific accomplishments, compared with the target you set in your APF.
5. Write your recommendations for next time.
6. Sign as "Prepared by" (organizer), and have your adviser, sponsor or coordinator sign as "Noted by".
7. Submit it to the SAU Chief.

The organizer, then the adviser, sponsor or coordinator.
