# Admission Slip

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Slip from the Registrar that lets you back into class after an absence, tardiness or cutting, and marks the absence excused or unexcused. It only counts once the Registrar signs it.

## Who needs it and when
Anyone who was absent, tardy or cut class, or just came back from representing the school. Absences count as excused for official school representation, sickness or a medical emergency, the death of an immediate family member, or similar reasons. Other reasons need advance approval from the Campus Director or an authorized official.

## Steps
1. Prepare an excuse letter signed by your parent or guardian, or a medical certificate acknowledged by your doctor.
2. Go to the [[Office - Registrar Unit]] for a printed slip, or print the blank form yourself. Fill in your basic info and the subjects you missed. Tick absent, tardy or cutting and write how many days, minutes or classes.
3. Go to the clinic ([[Office - Health Services Unit]]) or the [[Office - Guidance Unit]], whichever applies. It is usually the clinic. Get it stamped and approved.
4. Go back to the Registrar to get it stamped and marked excused or unexcused.
5. Go to each subject teacher whose class you missed and get their signature. Finish this within two days of coming back.
6. Once every signature is done, pass it to your adviser or class monitor.

## Who signs
1. Clinic (Health Services Unit) or Guidance Unit
2. Registrar (the slip is not valid without this)
3. Each subject teacher whose class you missed
4. Homeroom adviser (noted)

## Where to submit
Your adviser or class monitor, once all signatures are in.

## Template
Not online yet. The blank form waits for upload to the team Drive.

## Common mistakes
Waiting too long. You have two days after you come back to get every signature. After that, the absence counts as unexcused.
