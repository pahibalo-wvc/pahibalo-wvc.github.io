# Catch-Up Arrangement Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The SAU form where you plan how you will make up the classes and school events you miss for a co- or extra-curricular activity.

## Who needs it and when
Scholars who will miss classes or a calendared school event because of a co- or extra-curricular activity, like a competition or a training. One form can list several students from the same activity. File it before you leave.

%% "Before you leave" is inferred: the form asks for the dates of absence and an agreed plan and deadline, signed by the SAU Head. How it works with the [[Form - Student Representation Form]] (which the 2018 handbook names for the same case) is (unknown). %%

## Steps
1. Fill in your name, grade and section (and your groupmates' if you go as a team), the activity, the dates you will be absent, and your adviser or coach.
2. List each subject, course or calendared event you will miss, with the task or participation you will miss.
3. Have each subject teacher or event organizer sign beside their row.
4. Write the catch-up plan and the deadline you agreed on.
5. Sign it, and have your coach or adviser sign. Then the SAU Head signs.

## Who signs
Your subject teachers or the event organizers, your coach or adviser, you, and the SAU Head.

## Where to submit
The [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]].
