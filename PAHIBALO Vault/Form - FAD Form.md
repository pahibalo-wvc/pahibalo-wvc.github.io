# FAD Form (Permit to Use School Facilities)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The permit you file to use a school venue or borrow chairs, mics and other equipment for an activity. Your APF has to be approved first.

## Who needs it and when
Org officers, clubs, divisions or units that need a school venue or equipment for an activity. File it after your [[Form - Activity Proposal Form]] is approved.

## Steps
1. If your activity needs an APF, get it approved first.
2. Fill in the permit: your name and signature, your org, the activity, its purpose, and whether it is curricular, co-curricular or other.
3. Write the dates and times you need. Include setup day if you need one.
4. List the participants and how many are male and female.
5. Tick the venue: boardroom, audio-visual room, auditorium, gymnasium, training room, conference room, SLRC, grounds, dormitory, or others (write it in).
6. Tick the equipment and how many: chairs, tables, mics, whiteboards, LCD projector, electric fans, aircon units, trash bins, or others.
7. In Remarks, note that your APF is approved and have your adviser sign as "Noted by".
8. Get the signatures below, in order.

## Who signs
1. Your adviser (noted, in the Remarks box)
2. Division head concerned (recommending approval). For a student org this was the CID Chief.
3. Supervising Admin Officer, FAD Chief (approves)

## Where to submit
The FAD Officer.

## Common mistakes
Security, janitorial and IT help are not on this form. File a [[Form - Request for Services]] for those.
