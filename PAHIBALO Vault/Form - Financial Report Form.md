# Financial Report Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The yearly money report of an org or ALA: funds at the start, income, expenses and funds left. The treasurer prepares it and it goes to the SAU Chief.

## Who needs it and when
Treasurers of student orgs and ALAs, once at the start of the school year. An org also needs last year's financial report to get reaccredited (see [[Form - Org Accreditation Checklist]]).

For the money of one income generating project, use the [[Form - IGP Financial Report Form]] instead.

## Steps
1. Write the name of the org or ALA and the school year of the report.
2. Fill in the operating funds left from the previous school year.
3. List the operating income: membership fees, and ways and means like an income generating activity. Add the total.
4. List the operating expenses for each sponsored activity. Add the total.
5. Write the operating funds left at the end of the school year.
6. Sign as treasurer (prepared by), then get the president's signature (certified by) and the adviser's (noted by).
7. Submit it to the SAU Chief.
