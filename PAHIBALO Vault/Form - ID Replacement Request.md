# ID Replacement Request

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The Application for New ID you fill out when you lose your school ID. Bring an ID photo.

## Who needs it and when
Anyone who lost their ID. No ID, no entry at the main gate. You also need an updated ID to borrow library books.

## Steps
1. Fill out the Application for New ID: your name, birthday, ID number and school year.
2. Add an emergency contact: name, address and contact number.
3. Sign inside the box.
4. Paste an ID photo in the photo box.
5. Pay the fee at the cashier. Amount: (unknown).
6. Submit it to the [[Office - Registrar Unit]].
7. The 2018 handbook says the new ID is ready after three working days.

## Who signs
(unknown)

## Where to submit
[[Office - Registrar Unit]] (per the 2018 handbook).

## Common mistakes
Lending, borrowing or putting stickers on your ID is not allowed.
