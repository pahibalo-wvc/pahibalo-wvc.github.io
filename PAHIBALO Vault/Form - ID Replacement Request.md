# ID Replacement Request

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The Application for New ID you file at the MIS office when you need a new school ID. It costs P75, and a lost ID also needs an affidavit of loss.

## Who needs it and when
Anyone who lost their ID. No ID, no entry at the main gate. You also need an updated ID to borrow library books.

## Steps
1. Get the Application for New ID from the [[Office - Registrar Unit]], or print the blank form.
2. Fill it out: your name, birthday, ID number and school year.
3. Add an emergency contact: name, address and contact number.
4. Sign inside the box.
5. Paste an ID photo in the photo box.
6. If you lost your ID, get an affidavit of loss.
7. Pay the P75 fee at the cashier.
8. Submit the form, with the affidavit of loss if you lost your ID, to the [[Office - Management Information Systems]] (MIS). The Registrar gives out the form, but the MIS makes the ID.
9. The 2018 handbook says the new ID is ready after three working days.

## Who signs
(unknown)

## Where to submit
[[Office - Management Information Systems]] (MIS), not the Registrar.

## Common mistakes
Handing the filled-out form back to the Registrar. You get the form there, but you submit it to the MIS.

Forgetting the affidavit of loss when the ID is lost.

Lending, borrowing or putting stickers on your ID is not allowed.
