# IGP Financial Report Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The money report for one income generating project (IGP): what you collected, what you spent, and the profit.

## Who needs it and when
The organizer of an [[Event - Income Generating Projects|income generating project]], like a class food stall or a batch merch sale. File it after the IGP ends.

%% "After the IGP ends" is inferred: the form asks for the period of the IGP and its total profit. The exact deadline is not printed. %%

## Steps
1. Write the name or title of the IGP and the period it ran.
2. Fill in the funds left from your previous IGP, if any.
3. List the money collected, like membership fees or contributions.
4. List each expense and add the total.
5. Write the total revenue and the total profit.
6. Sign as "Prepared by" (organizer), and have your adviser sign as "Noted/Approved by".
7. Submit it to the SAU Chief.

%% The form prints "Submitted to: DSA Chief"; the page says SAU Chief, the same as the APF and the yearly Financial Report Form (PAHIBALO team, 2026-11-24). %%

## Who signs
The organizer, then the adviser.

## Where to submit
The SAU Chief at the [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]].
