# Incident Report

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Report of a possible Code of Conduct violation, filed with the Student Conduct Office. It used to be called the Anecdotal Report.

## Who needs it and when
Anyone in the school community who sees, finds or hears of a possible violation. File it as soon as you can.

## Steps
1. Write your name and your position or year and section.
2. Write the name and position or year and section of the person the report is about.
3. Name the offense. Check the [[Guide - Code of Conduct]] for the right term.
4. Write the date, time and place of the incident, and list any other witnesses.
5. Tell what happened in the narrative part. Be specific, and use an extra sheet if you need to.
6. List any pictures or documents you are attaching, and any interventions already done.
7. Sign it and hand it to the [[Office - Student Conduct Office]]. The office fills in the date, time and who received it.

%% Steps 1 to 7 inferred from the blank form (PSHS-00-F-SDO-01, "ANECDOTAL REPORT", in the team Drive). PAHIBALO team will comment on wrong ones. %%

You can also file it online through the Google Form, and you will get a PDF confirmation by email. The paper form still says "Anecdotal Report" at the top.

Under the 2025 code, staff who see or receive a report must tell the Student Conduct Officer within one school day. The 2013 code gave three school days to file.

## Who signs
The person filing. If a minor infraction was already handled on the spot, the staff member who handled it also signs.

## Where to submit
[[Office - Student Conduct Office]], second floor of the old academic building.
