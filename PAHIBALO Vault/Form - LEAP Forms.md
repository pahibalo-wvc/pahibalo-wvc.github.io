# LEAP Forms (formerly SCALE)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The four forms for the Leadership Advocacy Program (LEAP): personal information sheet, program proposal, activity plan and program report. The paper copies still say SCALE.

## Who needs it and when
Grade 11 and 12 scholars doing [[Guide - LEAP]] activities. Form 1 comes first, Forms 2 and 3 before each activity, and Form 4 after.

## The four forms
Form 1, the Personal Information Sheet, asks for your clubs and positions inside and outside PSHS, sports, instruments, arts and crafts, hobbies, activities you want to learn, and activities you have led. You sign it.

Form 2, the Program Proposal Form, is a table of all your planned activities: title, strand (S, C, A or L), type (I for individual, G for group), and target start and end month. Your LEAP adviser reviews it and marks it approved or for revision, and the LEAP coordinator notes it.

Form 3, the Individual Activity Plan, is filled out once per activity. It covers the description, objectives, people involved (adult supervisors and up to five other scholars), materials and costs, and a risk assessment of hazards and safety steps. Your parent signs a certification, then the adult supervisor and your LEAP adviser approve it.

Form 4, the Individual Program Report, comes after the activity. It lists what you learned, the date you finished, and your evidence: a narrative report, reflection paper, photos or videos, or a certification. Your adviser approves it.

## Steps
1. Fill out Form 1 once, at the start.
2. List your planned activities on Form 2 and hand it to your LEAP adviser.
3. Before each activity, fill out Form 3 and get your parent, the adult supervisor and your adviser to sign.
4. Do the activity and keep evidence: photos, a reflection or a certificate.
5. Fill out Form 4 with your evidence and get your adviser's approval.

## Who signs
You, your parent or guardian (Form 3), the adult supervisor (Form 3), and your LEAP adviser (Forms 2, 3 and 4). The LEAP coordinator notes Form 2.

## Where to submit
Your LEAP adviser.
