# Form A (Research Work Permit)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Permit to do research work off campus, on campus after class hours, or both. Your parent, research adviser and the CID Chief sign it, and the guard checks it.

## Who needs it and when
Research groups who need to work off campus (at an agency, lab or institution), stay on campus after class hours, or both. File it before the work date. Tick whichever applies at the top: off hours, off campus or on campus.

You also need a signed [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF) for this work, on top of the parent's permission part of Form A.

For an ordinary lab session during class hours, see the [[Form - Laboratory Use Form]].

## Your research code
The form asks for a research code number made of three parts: your research level (R1, R2 or R3), your work unit number (WU 01, 02 and so on), and your member number (1, 2, 3).

## Steps
1. Step 1 on the form: fill in the work details. That is the date, time slot, venue, mode of transport (public utility vehicle or private car), the agency's full address and contact number, what you plan to do there, and your adult companion or supervisor with their position, signature and number.
2. Sign it, and have your research adviser sign that the details are correct.
3. Step 2: your parent signs the permission part and writes a contact number.
4. Step 3: your research adviser signs that they know about the work and approve it, then the CID Chief signs.
5. Step 4: show it to the guard on duty, who signs it together with your research adviser.
6. Step 5, for off-campus work only: after the work, the agency staff who supervised you sign that it was done.

## Rules printed with the form
- Fill out every entry honestly, and write NA where something does not apply.
- Write names in full and dates as month/dd/yyyy.
- The destination must be specific. For a field or off-campus site, give coordinates or the complete address.
- A legal guardian who signs must be declared to the school, with a notarized document.
- Give every signatory a copy of the form. One copy goes to the research office, in the folder or box set out for it.
- The "authority" is the person at the venue who can vouch for your research work.
- Tell your parents and every signatory how you will handle and transport materials, and your safety measures, before asking for their signature.
- List the risks you found for the research activity.
- The form puts four older forms into one.

## Who signs
Your research adviser (twice), your parent, the CID Chief, the guard on duty, and for off-campus work the agency staff.

## Where to submit
Your research adviser, then the guard on duty on the day itself.
