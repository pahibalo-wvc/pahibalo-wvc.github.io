# Laboratory Request and Equipment Accountability Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Form to borrow laboratory materials or equipment, like glassware, for an experiment. The SRA records each item when it goes out and checks it when it comes back.

## Who needs it and when
Students or teachers who need materials or equipment for an experiment. The 2021 virtual campus tour says the chemistry stockroom is where you request glassware and equipment. Chemicals need the [[Form - Reagent Request Form]] instead.

## What the form asks for
School year, grade level and section, number of students, subject, the topic you are on, the unit, teacher in charge, where the experiment will be done, the date or dates and the time of use. Then a list of each item with its quantity and description. If a group will use the items, list up to five names.

The SRA fills in the condition of each item when it is issued and again when it is returned.

## Steps
1. Ask your teacher or research adviser what you need and for which day.
2. Fill in the details and list each item with its quantity. Leave the control number and the condition columns blank.
3. Sign as the requester and write the date you are filing it.
4. Have your subject teacher or unit head sign as endorser.
5. Hand it to the SRA of the unit during office hours. The SRS or SRA approves it.
6. When you get the items, sign "Received by" with the date.
7. Return everything after use. The SRA inspects the items, writes their condition, and signs "Received and inspected by". Year-end clearance checks that borrowed lab materials are back.

## Rules printed with the form
- Fill it out completely and legibly, and deal with the SRA of the unit during office hours.
- Requests that do not follow the unit's rules may not be granted.

## Who signs
You, as the requester. Your subject teacher or unit head endorses it. The SRS or SRA approves it and signs again when the items come back.

## Where to submit
The SRA of the unit: the Chemistry Stockroom, the Environmental Sciences Laboratory or the Biology Stockroom. The SRA offices are near the stockrooms.
