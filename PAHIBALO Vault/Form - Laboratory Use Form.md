# Laboratory Reservation Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Form to reserve a laboratory room for a date and time. Your subject teacher or unit head endorses it, and the SRS or SRA of the laboratory approves it.

## Who needs it and when
Students or teachers who need a laboratory room for lab or research work. The 2021 virtual campus tour says Chemistry Laboratory 1 and 2 are reserved at the chemistry stockroom, first come, first served, so file it early.

Research work off campus, or on campus after class hours, needs [[Form - Lab Form A]] (the research work permit) instead. To borrow materials or equipment for the session, also file the [[Form - Laboratory Request Form]].

## What the form asks for
School year, grade level and section, number of students, subject, teacher in charge, the date or dates, the time you will use the room, and your preferred lab room. If a group will use the lab, list up to five names.

## Steps
1. Pick the lab room, the date and the time slot. Ask the SRA early, since slots are first come, first served.
2. Fill in the details above. Leave the control number blank.
3. Sign as the requester and write the date you are filing it.
4. Have your subject teacher or unit head sign as endorser.
5. Hand it to the SRS or SRA of that laboratory, who approves the slot.
6. After the session, clean up and return any equipment you used.

## Who signs
You, as the requester. Your subject teacher or unit head endorses it. The SRS or SRA approves it.

## Where to submit
The SRS or SRA of the laboratory you are using: the Chemistry Stockroom, the Environmental Sciences Laboratory or the Biology Stockroom. The SRA offices are near the stockrooms.

Form code PSHS-00-F-CID-05.
