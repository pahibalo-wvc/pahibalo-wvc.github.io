# Laundry Slip

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The slip that goes with your laundry to your laundry woman. It keeps a record of every piece you send, so nothing gets lost.

## Who needs it and when
Dormers, each time they send laundry to their laundry woman. Interns wash only light garments themselves (see [[Dorm - Dorm Rules]]), so the slip is for everything else.

## Steps
1. Write the date, your name, your grade and section, and the name of your laundry woman.
2. Write how many of each item you are sending: T-shirt, bath towel, face towel, socks, handkerchief, shorts, maong pants, slacks, jogging pants, sando, girls' blouse and skirt (uniform), half slip, coat or jacket, bedsheets, blankets, pillow cases, or others (write it in).
3. Hand your laundry and the slip to your laundry woman.
4. You and the laundry woman both sign under Checked.
5. When your clean laundry comes back, check it against the slip to make sure nothing is missing.

The 2018 security rules say laundry women may enter campus at 4:30 PM Monday to Thursday and 4:00 PM on Friday.
