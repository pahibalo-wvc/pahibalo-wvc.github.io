# Leave Pass

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Pass a dormer needs to leave campus.

## Who needs it and when
Dormers (interns) need a leave pass to go off campus, and there are three kinds. A regular leave pass is for short trips, like buying supplies, or for going out on weekends and holidays. A home leave pass is for going home to your parents or guardian on Wednesdays or weekends.

An official leave pass is for school functions such as seminars, field trips and recollections. For this one, you have to show your parent's consent.
