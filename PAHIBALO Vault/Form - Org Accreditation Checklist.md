# Org Accreditation Checklist

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The checklist of papers a new org or ALA needs to get accredited, and the one for getting reaccredited each year.

## Who needs it and when
Officers starting a new org or ALA, and officers of an existing one renewing its accreditation. When in the year to file is (unknown).

## Steps for a new org or ALA
Prepare these papers:
1. Application letter
2. Constitution and by-laws
3. Program or calendar of activities
4. Your adviser's acceptance letter
5. List of officers, with their latest student records
6. List of members

## Steps for reaccreditation
Prepare these papers:
1. Reaccreditation request letter
2. Program or calendar of activities
3. Your adviser's acceptance letter
4. Accomplishment report from last school year (see [[Form - Activity Report Form]])
5. Financial report from last school year (see [[Form - Financial Report Form]])
6. List of officers, with their latest student records
7. List of members

[Reaccreditation checklist on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1p59ZKHGDU10d8zzYVake1J-LJoAEqG2l/view).

## Who signs
The club or ALA coordinator (or the Assistant Division Chief for Student Affairs) reviews it, and the DSA Chief or CID Chief approves it.

## Where to submit
The [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]], which accredits orgs and ALAs.
