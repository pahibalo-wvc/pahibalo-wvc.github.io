# Parent Consent Form (PCF)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The PCF. Your parent or guardian signs it for most or all activities held off campus or outside class hours, and for activities that take you out of class.

## Who needs it and when
The Parent Consent Form (PCF) is the form filed for most or all activities held off campus or outside class hours. Students also file it for an activity that takes them out of class. Each activity gives out its own copy, so the wording changes from one activity to the next. The example we have is for the Batch 2027 [[Event - Yearbook Creative Shots]].

Research work off campus or after class hours needs [[Form - Lab Form A]] and a PCF. Form A has its own part for your parent's permission, but the PCF is still filed.

## Steps
1. Get the form from the activity's organizer.
2. Fill in the date, your name and section. The activity title, venue, date and time are usually already filled in.
3. Your parent or guardian signs and adds their contact number and the date.
4. Submit it to the organizer or teacher-adviser named on the form.

By signing, your parent agrees that a teacher-adviser will be with you and that you will get time to make up missed requirements.

## Who signs
Your parent or guardian.

## Where to submit
The organizer or teacher-adviser named on the form.

## Template
The blank PCF is on the SAU Forms folder on Google Drive. Organizers fill in the activity details before they hand it out.
