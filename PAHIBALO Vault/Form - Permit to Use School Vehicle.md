# Permit to Use School Vehicle

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Request to use a school vehicle for a trip, like going to a competition or off-campus activity.

## Who needs it and when
Anyone who needs a school vehicle for a school trip. Students usually go through their adviser or coach. Who may file it directly: (unknown).

Students on the trip will usually also need a signed [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF), since the trip is off campus.

## Steps
1. Write the vehicle you are requesting.
2. Fill in the trip date, number of passengers, destinations, departure and arrival times, and purpose.
3. Sign as the requisitioner and write your position.
4. Get the signatures below.

## Who signs
1. Division Chief (recommends approval or disapproval)
2. FAD Chief (approves or disapproves)

## Where to submit
The FAD Officer.
