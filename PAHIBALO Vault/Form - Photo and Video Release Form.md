# Photo and Video Release Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The form for your science immersion: it says whether your host agency may use photos and videos of you, and for how long.

## Who needs it and when
Scholars going on the Science Immersion Program (SIP), before the immersion starts.

%% The handbook and the site call the summer program the Science Internship Program; this form and the SAU calendar (SIP Orientation 2027) call it the Science Immersion Program. They are most likely the same program under a newer name (inferred). The grade tag (G9 to G11) follows [[Guide - Science Internship Program]]. %%

## What you agree to
- The host agency may use your photos and videos in its publications and other media during the SIP.
- It may use them only during the SIP, or up to 6 months from the start of your immersion.
- Anything related to intellectual property is left out.
- You will not ask for money for their use.
- You can also tick that you do not give permission.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, the photos and videos are only for SIP purposes and may not be shared without written consent from the PSHS System and from you.

## Steps
1. Write the name of your SIP host agency in each blank.
2. Tick whether you agree or do not give permission.
3. Sign with your printed name, the date and your campus.
4. Have your parent or guardian sign and date it.

## Who signs
You, then your mother, father or guardian.
