# Project and Risk Management Plan

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The SAU plan for how your activity will run and stay safe: goals, schedule, budget, risks, and who supervises.

## Who needs it and when
Students running an activity, whether it is extra- or co-curricular, on or off campus, for a subject, ALA, homeroom or org. It tells the student organizers, the adult supervisors and the school how the activity will be done. Which activities must attach it, and when, is (unknown).

## Steps
1. Write the proponents, grade and section, date, activity title, and the subject, ALA, homeroom or org.
2. Tick the type: extra- or co-curricular, and on or off campus.
3. Overview: write the objectives, the schedule and program flow, the budget, and the people, places and equipment you need, with each member's role.
4. Management: list the stakeholders, each risk with how you will prevent or handle it, how your team will communicate, and how you will measure success.
5. Post-event: write your plan for clean-up, returning borrowed facilities, damage or incident reports, and evaluation.
6. Write your teacher-adviser or supervisor and tick how they will supervise: on site, online, or through an outside adult supervisor (name them and their roles).
7. Sign as "Prepared by", and have your teacher-adviser or supervisor sign as "Noted by".

## Who signs
The proponents, then the teacher-adviser or supervisor.
