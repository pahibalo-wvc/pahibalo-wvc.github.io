# Quarterly Wellness Feedback Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> A short survey each quarter about the school's Wellness Program: the daily 30-minute wellness period and the monthly wellness session.

## Who needs it and when
Scholars, once a quarter. The school uses the answers for its quarterly report on the "30 for Wellness" program.

## What the Wellness Program is
- A daily 30-minute wellness period. Classes and other activities should not use this time.
- A monthly 30-minute wellness session.
- Activities of five kinds: physical (sports, exercise), mental (reflection, journaling), social (peer circles), spiritual (quiet reflection) and creative (art, music, writing).
- Quiet zones like the library and wellness rooms, plus sports, art and open spaces.
- A campus Wellness Committee. Student leaders in the ALP, LEAP and the [[Org - Student Alliance]] help promote it, and you can ask PEHM teachers, counselors or life coaches for support.

## Steps
1. Write your name and section.
2. Answer 20 items: how well the wellness period is kept, which activities you join, the spaces, supervision, support, and how satisfied you are.
3. Write what new activities you want, what works well, and what to improve.
