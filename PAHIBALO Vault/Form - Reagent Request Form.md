# Reagent Request Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Form to request chemicals and reagents for an experiment. You read each reagent's Safety Data Sheet first and hand the form in with a labeled container that has a cover.

## Who needs it and when
Students or teachers who need chemicals or reagents for an experiment. The 2021 virtual campus tour says the chemistry stockroom is where you request chemicals and reagents. Glassware and equipment need the [[Form - Laboratory Request Form]] instead.

## What the form asks for
School year, grade level and section, number of students, subject, the topic you are on, the unit, teacher in charge, where the experiment will be done, the date or dates and the time of use. Then each reagent with its quantity, and a column to show you checked its Safety Data Sheet (SDS). If a group will use the reagents, list up to five names.

The SRA fills in the amount issued and any remarks.

## Steps
1. List the reagents you need and the amount of each. Ask your teacher if you are unsure of the concentration.
2. Read the Safety Data Sheet of every reagent on your list. The form asks you to certify that you did.
3. Fill in the details. Leave the control number and the issued amount blank.
4. Sign as the requester and write the date you are filing it.
5. Have your subject teacher or unit head sign as endorser.
6. Bring the form with a suitable container that has a cover and a proper label, and hand both to the SRA of the unit that releases the reagents. The SRS or SRA of that unit approves it.
7. The reagents are released to the SRA of your unit, not to you directly. Get them from your unit's SRA and sign "Received by".

## Rules printed with the form
- You must certify that you read the safety information in the Safety Data Sheet of each reagent you request.
- Fill it out completely and legibly. Submit it with a suitable container with a cover and a proper label to the SRA of the unit that will release the reagents.
- Requests that do not follow the unit's rules may not be granted.
- The reagents are released to the SRA of the requesting unit.

## Who signs
You, as the requester. Your subject teacher or unit head endorses it. The SRS or SRA of the releasing unit approves it.

## Where to submit
The SRA of the unit that keeps the reagent, usually the Chemistry Stockroom. The SRA offices are near the stockrooms.
