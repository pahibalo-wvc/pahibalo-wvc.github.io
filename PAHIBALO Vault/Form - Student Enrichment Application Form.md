# Student Enrichment Application Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Application to join a student enrichment activity, like an outside competition. You and your parent sign it, then hand it to the DSA Chief or CID Chief.

## Who needs it and when
Scholars applying to join an enrichment activity, like a competition, camp or training outside school.

There is a second version for competitions the school does not run. On that one, your parent agrees that the event is not officially sanctioned by the school, that no teacher or staff member will go as a chaperone, and that your family is fully responsible for your safety. The school only gives recognition and coaching. [Competition version on Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1wdyHInFRbKw0KZTHHZjDHjdR3f46QmEH/view).

If the activity is off campus or outside class hours, you will usually also file a [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF), signed by your parent or guardian.

%% "Like a competition, camp or training" is inferred from the competition version of the form and from the SAU calendar (olympiads, camps and trainings filed with the SAU). Which activities need the form is not printed on it. %%

## Steps
1. Write the title of the activity.
2. Fill in your name, birthdate, section, mobile number, home address and scholarship category.
3. Write your father's and mother's names and mobile numbers.
4. Tick whether you are willing to pay for the activity's expenses, and write what you think you can get from joining.
5. Sign as "Prepared by", and have your parent or guardian sign as "Concurred by".
6. Submit it to the DSA Chief or CID Chief, who dates it.

## Who signs
You (prepared by) and your parent or guardian (concurred by).

## Where to submit
The CID Chief.

## Template
On the SAU Forms folder on Google Drive.
