# Student Representation Form

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Form you fill before missing class to represent the school.

## Who needs it and when
Any student joining a competition or outside activity that makes them miss class. File it three school days before the activity.

If the activity is off campus or outside class hours, you will usually also file a [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF), signed by your parent or guardian.

## Steps
1. Write the title and date of the activity, your name and your section.
2. Tell your class monitor which days you will miss.
3. List each subject you will miss. Each subject teacher writes the requirement you will miss and signs to agree (conforme).
4. Sign as "Prepared by", and have your HR or academic adviser sign as "Noted by".
5. Submit it to the CID Chief three school days before the activity.
6. During the event you are marked "On Competition", not absent.
7. When back, get an [[Form - Admission Slip]] from the Registrar.
8. Finish missed work within five school days. Each teacher writes the date and signs the last column when you are done.

## Who signs
Your subject teachers, then your HR or academic adviser.

## Where to submit
The CID Chief.
