# Year-End Clearance

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Clearance every student completes at year end to show no pending obligations.

## Who needs it and when
Every student, at the end of each school year.

## Steps
1. Before clearance week, return library books, lab materials and anything else you borrowed. Claim all your allowances and settle any payments at the cashier.
2. If your GWA is below 2.25, go to the Guidance Unit for a guidance conference first.
3. Get your clearance signed by each office listed under Who signs. The school's Clearance Pro system supports this (see [[Kit - School Online Systems]]).
4. Dormers also need the Residence Halls Head's signature, and Grade 12 scholars need the Campus Director's.
5. Submit the finished clearance. Where to submit it is (unknown).
(inferred from the 2018 handbook)

## Who signs
Discipline Officer · Science Lab Technician · Computer Lab Technician · Cashier · Supply and Property Officer · Guidance Counselor · Librarian · Residence Halls Head · Physician/Nurse · Homeroom Adviser · ALA/Club Adviser · SCALE Adviser (G11-12) · Division Chiefs · Campus Director (G12 only).
