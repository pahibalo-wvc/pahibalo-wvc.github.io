# Alternative Learning Activities

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Required weekly clubs for Grades 7 to 10.

- Grades 7-10 must join an ALA (one club, once a week). Grades 11-12 may join orgs optionally.
- Grades 7-10 join one to three orgs or ALAs.
- Offerings change every year. 2018 list included: Anime, Stellar, Sports, Photography, Volleyball, ICT and Robotics, Martial Arts, Social Transformers, Performing Arts, Public Speaking, League of Science Scholars, Kaibigan (peer support), 3-D Modelling and Game Development, Student Broadsheets, K-Pop, Math ALA.
- New clubs need SAU accreditation and at least 15 members.
- ALP (Alternative Learning Programs): (unknown).
