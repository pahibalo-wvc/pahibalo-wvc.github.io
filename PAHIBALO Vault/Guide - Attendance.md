# Attendance

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> How tardiness, absences and cutting are counted.

You are counted tardy if you are not in line when the Monday flag ceremony starts, or if you come to class 10 or more minutes late. Cutting means you are on campus but not in class, or you left class and did not come back within 10 minutes.

You are absent if you missed all your classes in a day, or a school-organized activity. Being absent four or more days in one quarter counts as absenteeism.

After any of these, you need an [[Form - Admission Slip]], which is also how an absence gets marked excused. Get it done within two days of coming back.

The flag ceremony is on Mondays from 7:30 to 8:00 AM at the gym. Flag lowering is on Fridays at 4:00 PM at the flag pole.
