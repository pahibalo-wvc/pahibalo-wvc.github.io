# Curriculum and Units

> Subjects and units per grade (matrix revised January 2015).

## Phases
Grades 7-8 Foundation · Grades 9-10 Advancement · Grades 11-12 Specialization.

## Units rule
6 meetings a week = 2 units · 5 = 1.7 · 4 = 1.3 · 3 = 1 · 2 = 0.7

## Units per grade
Grades 8 and 9 follow the newer curriculum, read from current report cards (SY 2025-2026 for Grade 8, SY 2026-2027 for Grade 9). The other grades still use the 2015 matrix.
- **G7 (10.4):** Integrated Science 1 1.7 · Math 1 1.7 · English 1 1.3 · Filipino 1, Social Science 1, PEHM 1, AdTech 1, Computer Science 1 (1 each) · Values Ed 1 0.7 (Complete, not in GWA)
- **G8 (13.0):** AdTech 2, Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science 2, Earth Science, Filipino 2, Math 2 (Algebra 2), Math 3 (Geometry), PEHM 2, Physics, Social Science 2 (1 each) · English 2 1.3 · Values Ed 2 0.7 (Complete, not in GWA)
- **G9 (11.6):** Biology 2 1.3 · Chemistry 2 1.3 · Physics 2 1.3 · Computer Science 3, English 3, Filipino 3, Math 3 (Algebra 3), PEHM 3, Social Science 3, Statistics 1 (1 each) · Values Ed 3 0.7 (Complete, not in GWA)

## How the report card computes the GWA
- Values Education has no number grade. It is marked Complete or Incomplete, and completing it is needed to graduate. Its units count in the card's total units but not in the GWA.
- GWA = sum of (grade × units) ÷ sum of units of the graded subjects.
- The card cuts the GWA to two decimals instead of rounding it. A GWA of 1.5298 shows as 1.52.
- Grading is cumulative, so the final grade of a subject is its fourth-quarter grade.
