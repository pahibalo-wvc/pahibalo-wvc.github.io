# Honors and Awards

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Director's List, graduation honors and excellence awards.

You make the Director's List with a GWA of 1.00 to 1.50 in a quarter. The list is posted within five days after card day.

At graduation, a six-year GWA of 1.00 to 1.20 earns With Highest Honors, and 1.21 to 1.50 earns With High Honors. Both need every final grade to be 2.50 or better and at least four years at PSHS. The GWA is truncated after two decimals, not rounded.

Excellence Awards are given per subject. You need at least 1.50 in that subject every year you took it, plus points from competitions and activities.

All of these also need a clean record from Grade 7 to 12. The 2018 handbook says no Level III offense (a level from the old 2013 code) and no academic dishonesty.
