# LEAP (formerly SCALE)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The Leadership Advocacy Program (LEAP): the non-graded Grade 11 and 12 program of service, creativity, action and leadership activities. It used to be called SCALE.

## What it is
SCALE stood for Service, Creativity, Action, and Leadership Enhancement. It is a non-graded program for Grades 11 and 12 under the 2015 curriculum framework. It is now called LEAP, the Leadership Advocacy Program. Its current requirements are (unknown).

Each activity you plan falls under one or more strands: Service, Creativity, Action or Leadership. It can be an individual activity or a group activity with up to five other scholars.

## What you hand in
The forms come in a set of four, from your personal information sheet to the final report. The steps are on [[Form - LEAP Forms]]. Your LEAP adviser reviews each activity, and a LEAP coordinator notes the program proposal.

## Clearance
The LEAP (SCALE) adviser signs Grade 11 and 12 [[Form - Year-End Clearance]].
