# Representing the School

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Rules for competing or attending outside events.

- The Campus Director approves, through your coach.
- To miss class for it: GWA not below 2.25; no 2.75, 3.00, 4.00 or 5.00 last quarter; not on probation. A 2.50 needs an appeal letter and a conference with parents, coach, adviser and teacher.
- File the [[Form - Student Representation Form]] 3 school days before. You are marked "On Competition".
- Back at school: [[Form - Admission Slip]]; make up work within 5 school days.
- Off-campus trips need your parent's permit, the [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF), and 1 teacher per 15 students.
