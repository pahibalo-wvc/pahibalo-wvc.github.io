# SA Locate (lost and found)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Lost something on campus, or found something. Post it in SA Locate, the Student Alliance's lost-and-found Google Chat.

## How it works
SA Locate is a Google Chat space for lost and found items. There is no paper form for it anymore.

How to join the chat: (unknown). The rules for posting and for claiming an item: (unknown).

## Older rules that may still apply
The 2018 handbook said unclaimed items after the last day of classes are donated to charity through the [[Org - Student Alliance]]. Lost school books go to the Property Office, and getting them back needs a parent conference and a commitment paper. Whether these still apply is (unknown).
