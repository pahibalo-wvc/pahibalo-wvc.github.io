# Scholarship Policy

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Every scholar gets free tuition, a stipend and textbook loans for six years.

## What you get
- Free tuition, monthly stipend, loan of textbooks.
- Monthly living allowance for those who qualify by socio-economic status.
- Categories: Full, Partial 1, Partial 2, Partial 3.

## What you agree to
- The grant lasts six years unless ended early.
- After graduating, take a science and technology course from the approved list, or repay the value of the scholarship.
- Willfully abandoning the scholarship also means repaying it.
