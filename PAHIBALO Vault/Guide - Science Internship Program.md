# Science Internship Program

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Required 80-hour summer internship.

- Required, non-graded. At least 80 hours, in a summer after Grade 9 (proposed: offered to incoming G10 or G11).
- Lab work, research, design, field work, programming or journal review at a partner institution, possibly abroad.
- Formerly the Summer Science Internship Program (SSIP). The handbook heading calls it the Science Immersion Program.
