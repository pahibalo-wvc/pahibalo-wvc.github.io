# Standing Probation and Termination

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> What keeps you in good standing, and what puts the scholarship at risk.

## Good standing
You stay in good standing with a final GWA of 2.25 or better, no 5.00 in the final quarter, and three or fewer unexcused absences.

## Probation (next school year)
You are put on probation for the next school year if your GWA falls below 2.25 or you have eight or more unexcused absences. The 2018 handbook also lists a Level III or higher offense, which is a level from the old 2013 code. On probation, you cannot be an org officer or represent the school.

## Termination (Grades 7 to 11)
The 2018 handbook lists four grounds for termination: a 5.00 in the final quarter, 13 or more unexcused absences, two probations in a row, and two Level III or higher offenses in a year. Under the 2025 [[Guide - Code of Conduct]], conduct-based termination can be recommended after three major infractions or one grave infraction.
