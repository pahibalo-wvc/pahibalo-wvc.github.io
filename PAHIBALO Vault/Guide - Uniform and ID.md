# Uniform and ID

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> What to wear and the ID rules.

Wear your ID at all times on campus, since you cannot enter the gate without it. Do not lend it or put stickers on it. If you lose it, file an [[Form - ID Replacement Request]].

The boys' uniform is a white polo with the PSHS patch over a white undershirt, black pants, white or black socks and black leather shoes. The girls' uniform is a cream blouse with the patch over a white undershirt, a brown-and-white checkered skirt, white socks and black leather shoes.

Your homeroom adviser checks haircuts on the first Monday of each month. Off campus, the 2018 handbook says to wear the uniform only for school purposes.
