The hub lists five subject pages: Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics, Physics and Humanities. Each one says how to get in and links its own contests (pages with a `parent`). STEM contests go through subject pools: you join a pool by passing its selection exam or by a teacher's recommendation. Journalism takes a teacher's recommendation, membership in Banaag or Sci-Link, or a passed screening.

Rules for all competitions: [[Guide - Representing the School]]. For a competition off campus, you will usually file a [[Form - Student Representation Form]] and a [[Form - Parent Consent Form]] (PCF). PubliSCIENCE sits here because it is where senior research gets published.
