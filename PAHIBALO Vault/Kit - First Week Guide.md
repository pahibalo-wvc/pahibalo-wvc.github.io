## Goal
Get through week one without surprises.

## Things to know
1. Wear your ID at all times. No ID, no entry. → [[Guide - Uniform and ID]]
2. Monday flag ceremony starts 7:30 AM at the gym; not in line = tardy. → [[Guide - Attendance]]
3. If you are late or absent, get an admission slip from the Registrar. → [[Form - Admission Slip]]
4. Pick your ALA. → [[Guide - Alternative Learning Activities]]
5. Learn how grades work. → [[Guide - Grading System]]
