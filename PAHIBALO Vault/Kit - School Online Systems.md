# School Official Online Systems

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The school's own online systems at pshswvc.com, like Endeavor, Medifast and Clearance Pro, and what each one is for.

All of these sit on the info systems portal at pshswvc.com, which was built by the campus [[Office - Management Information Systems]]. The names below use the portal's own wording. Which ones students use day to day is (unknown), so the list is split by who the system seems to be for.

## Systems that touch students

| System | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Endeavor | School management system |
| Medifast | Reserving a slot at the clinic ([[Office - Health Services Unit]]) |
| Clearance Pro | Student clearance ([[Form - Year-End Clearance]]) |
| Accessway Plus | Student entry and exit records |
| Breakapp | Breaktime recording |
| Presence | Attendance monitoring |
| Ask | Online helpdesk |
