# Cooperative (Coop)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Where you buy meals, kakanin, snacks, drinks, toiletries and school supplies on campus. Open from about 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. GCash cash-out runs 9:00 AM to 2:30 PM.

## Hours
The Coop is open from about 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Exact times can change.

## Meals and drinks at the counter
The Coop sells rice meals (ulam) from trays at the counter, with pork, beef, chicken, fish and vegetable dishes. It also has an all-day breakfast list with hotdog, tocino, ham, corned beef, burger steak, eggs, lumpiang shanghai and soups.

Kakanin like puto and kutsinta sit in baskets by the counter, along with bread and other packed snacks. For drinks, there is bottled water, juice, calamansi, milk, Milo and coffee.

## Grocery shelves
The grocery side has big shelves of chips and biscuits. Candy and chocolate sit in clear bins at the counter, and some of it is sold by the piece, like 3 pieces for ₱5. On the side shelves you can find pens, hair ties, clips, toiletries and tissue rolls. A chiller holds cold drinks with its own price list.
