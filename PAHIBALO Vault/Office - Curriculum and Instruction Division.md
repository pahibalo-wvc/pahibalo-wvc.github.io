# Curriculum and Instruction Division

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The division over academics. Its chief chairs the Honors and Awards Committee and signs the forms for research permits, enrichment activities and representing the school.

## What it handles
Academics. The CID Chief chairs the Honors and Awards Committee and sits on the Scholarship Committee. CISD Chiefs can sign dormer passes.

## Where it is
Beside the new academic building and the boys' residence hall.

## What is on its bulletin board
The CID bulletin board has QR codes (and NFC tags) that open the list of HR, SCALE and ALP advisers, the list of electives and options, the class schedule, the calendar of activities, the process flow and references, the Code of Conduct, and the Citizen's Charter. Scan them there to get the current copies.
