# Finance and Administration Division

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The division over the finance and admin units, like cash, accounting, budget, supply and general services. Its chief approves the FAD Form, vehicle permits and requests for services.

## What it handles
Finance and admin units: cash, accounting, budget, supply and property, procurement, records, HR, general services (2018 org chart).

The 2021 virtual campus tour says the ground floor of the Administration Building has offices like budget and accounting, which give financial and administrative support to the whole school. The Campus Director's office is in the same building.

Likely owner of the [[Form - FAD Form]] (unconfirmed).
