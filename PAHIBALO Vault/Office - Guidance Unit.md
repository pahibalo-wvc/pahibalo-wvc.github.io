# Guidance Unit

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The office for counseling, homeroom guidance, career guidance and tests like the mental ability and learning style tests. Two school counselors split the grades between them.

## What it handles
Counseling, homeroom guidance, testing (mental ability, EQ, learning style, Holland code), career guidance, records, information service.

Students whose GWA falls below 2.25 need a guidance conference for clearance (2018).

## Where it is
The first floor of the new academic building. The room number is (unknown).

%% The tour frame was identified by order: the narration says the new academic building has the clinic and the guidance office, and this office is shown right after the clinic beds and dental chair. Flagged To proofread in the sheet. %%

## Who to see
There are two school counselors: one for Grades 7 to 9 and one for Grades 10 to 12. A guidance staff member also works in the office.

## When to go
Monday, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Tuesday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:30 PM. These hours are from the sign on the office door (2026).
