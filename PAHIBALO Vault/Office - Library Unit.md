# Library Unit

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Borrow books, book the discussion room, read research papers and use the computers. The library also has an air-conditioned lounge with bean bags.

## Borrow a book
1. Bring your updated school ID.
2. Fill out your library card (form PSHS-00-F-LIB-02). It asks for your name, year and section, address, student number and a 1x1 ID picture. Each book you borrow gets a line with the date borrowed, the date returned, and the book's call number and accession number.
3. Write your details in the library registration book.

The 2018 handbook says you can borrow up to four books (2 circulation for 3 days, 2 fiction for 1 week), and the borrower's card needs one 1x1 photo. These limits may have changed.

## Book the discussion room
1. Go to the library and ask the librarian.
2. Write your booking in the library's logbook.
3. Ask the librarian to check that no one else has the room at that time.

## What else it handles
- An air-conditioned lounge with bean bags, where you can read or rest.
- PSHS research papers (since 1996) are room use only and cannot be photocopied.
- Free internet for research; printing is paid.

## When to go
7:30 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Leave bags at the baggage counter. (2018)
