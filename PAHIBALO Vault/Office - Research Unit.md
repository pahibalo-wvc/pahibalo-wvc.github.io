# Research Unit

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The campus research office. It supports the research program and runs the PubliSCIENCE research site, and its laboratories are in the old academic building.

## What it handles
Research program support; presents publiscience.org. Details (unknown).

The office is on the first floor of the old academic building. From the Administration Building, turn left.

The 2021 virtual campus tour shows the research and biology laboratories on the ground floor of the old academic building: a culture and isolation laboratory and a molecular laboratory with a PCR thermal cycler, gel electrophoresis and a gel documentation system.
