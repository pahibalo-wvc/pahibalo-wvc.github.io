# Residence Halls Unit

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Runs the boys' and girls' residence halls, where dormers live four to a room. It handles dorm rules, supervised study, dorm activities and leave passes.

## What it handles
Dorm lodging, order and security, supervised study, dorm activities, leave passes.

There is a boys' residence hall (BRH) on the left side of the campus and a girls' residence hall (GRH) on the right side. Each room has four beds, four desks and four cabinets. In the 2021 virtual campus tour, each building has three stories with rooms for one to two people during the pandemic setup, and up to 108 dormers, four to a room, in the usual setup. Each hall has its own kitchen and play area.

See [[Dorm - Dorm Rules]].
