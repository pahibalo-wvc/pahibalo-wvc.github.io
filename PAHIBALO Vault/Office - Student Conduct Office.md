# Student Conduct Office (formerly Discipline Office)

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Handles conduct cases under the 2025 Code of Conduct. Students file Incident Reports here, and the Student Conduct Officer gives an intervention for each case.

## Where it is
The office is on the second floor of the old academic building.

## Contact
Email scocares@wvc.pshs.edu.ph. The old Discipline Office used disciplineoffice@wvc.pshs.edu.ph and a Google Sites portal, and whether these still work for the SCO is (unknown).

## What it handles
- Reports of infractions. Any school staff member who sees or hears of one tells the Student Conduct Officer (SCO) within one school day. Students file an [[Form - Incident Report]].
- The SCO is a teacher picked by the Campus Director. They look into reports, sort them as minor, major or grave, and give guiding interventions.
- Major and grave cases go up to the Student Conduct Committee (SCC) for a formal inquiry.
- Records of finished cases are kept here.
- Lost items now go through [[Guide - SA Locate]], a Google Chat. Whether the SCO still keeps found items is (unknown).

The full process is on [[Guide - Code of Conduct]].

## SCO Cares
The posters in the office explain how the SCO works with students. The "SCO Cares" framework has five parts: Character, Awareness, Responsibility, Empowerment and Safety.

Talks with the SCO follow the "Halika, usap tayo" CARE approach: Connect, Appreciate, Reflect and Empower. The office tagline is "Thrive. Lead. Succeed. Discipline is what you need."
