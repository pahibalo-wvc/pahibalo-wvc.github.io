## What it is
Banaag is a student broadsheet (school paper) of PSHS-WVC, and its posts are written in Filipino. The 2018 handbook also listed it as an ALA. Its old website (banaag.pshswvc.net) no longer works.

## What members do
Members write news and features and take photos of school events, then post them on the Banaag Facebook page. In November 2026 Banaag covered both days of [[Event - MaHuESci]] and the senior research presentations. Members also compete in the [[Comp - Schools Press Conference]], where the school was named first Top Performing School at the 2026 Division Schools Press Conference.

## When it is active
During the school year, whenever there is a school event to cover. Press conference season runs from the division round to the regional round (RSPC 2026 was in February 2026). In SY 2026-2027, Banaag and Sci-Link trained together for the Division Schools Press Conference, held on November 7 to 8, 2026.
