# COMELEC

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The campus Commission on Elections. It runs the yearly Student Alliance elections: filing of candidacy, the campaign, the Grand Rally and election day.

## What it is
COMELEC is the campus Commission on Elections. It runs the elections for the [[Org - Student Alliance]] every school year. Its motto is "Integrity in Governance, Transparency in Process, Democracy in Action", and each year's commission adds its own slogan. It posts on its Facebook page and also has a Google Site that needs a school sign-in. Email: comelec@wvc.pshs.edu.ph.

## What members do
Members run every step of the election and post each step on Facebook:
1. Filing of certificates of candidacy (COC). In 2026 it ran March 24 to 27, later extended to March 31. Candidates filed through a Google Form with a 2x2 ID picture.
2. Introducing the candidates. In 2026 two parties ran, each with a full slate of 18.
3. The campaign period (March 31 to April 20 in 2026), including a room-to-room campaign during homeroom.
4. The Grand Rally in the New Auditorium (April 20, 2026), with a debate, campaign speeches and an open forum. Students could send questions for the candidates ahead of time through a Google Form.
5. Election day (April 23, 2026; April 30 in 2025), then posting the results for each position, with the share of votes and abstentions.

The SA positions voted on include president, vice presidents for internal and external affairs, secretary and assistant secretary, treasurer and assistant treasurer, auditor, public relations officers, business managers, and one representative for each batch. From 2026, batch representatives run as part of the Student Alliance elections.
