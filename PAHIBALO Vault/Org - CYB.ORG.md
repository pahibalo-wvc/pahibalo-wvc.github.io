# CYB.ORG

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> PSHS-WVC's robotics org. It builds robots for science events and gets students started in robotics.

## What it is
CYB.ORG wants every student, whatever their background, to get a chance at robotics. The org builds robots and machines with kits like VEX IQ and Arduino, then presents them at science events. It also runs its own events and works with other orgs.

## What members do
See the committees below. From September 10 to 12, 2025, CYB.ORG ran a booth at the Regional Science and Technology Week (RSTW) in Robinsons Roxas City, Capiz. The booth had virtual reality demos with Meta Quest headsets and computer science projects by Pisay students, and a live robotics competition was streamed on the DOST Facebook page.

## Committees
The Technicals committee builds the robots and fixes them when the hardware or code fails. A background in computer science or robotics helps, but members can learn on the job.

The Programs committee plans each event and its program flow, including hosting and games, and helps run it on the day. Creatives makes the pubmats for the org's page, and Research fact-checks everything before it is posted. Public Relations works with other orgs, answers questions on the page and handles event paperwork.

The executive council has a president, vice president, secretary and assistant secretary, treasurer, auditor, public information officer and two business managers. Each committee also has its own head.

## How to join
CYB.ORG takes members through an application, then posts the results by committee on its Facebook page. When the application opens is (unknown).

## When it is active
During the school year, around science events like the RSTW in September. The 2026 RSTW was on October 1 to 3, 2026.
