# Cinematograpisay

Back to [[Home]]

## What it is
Cinematograpisay, or SineSci for short, is the official documentation team of PSHS-WVC. Its Facebook page posts the photos from school events, like "IN PHOTOS: Buwan ng Wika 2026". It is a good place to look for pictures of yourself after a big event.

## What members do
Members photograph and film school events such as [[Event - MaHuESci]], Intramurals, band performances and the [[Event - Acquaintance Party]]. They post photo albums and edit recap videos. At the end of 2024 the team ran a week-long "SineSci 2024 Recap" of the year's events. The team has a core team led by a director, and a photography committee with its own director of photography.

## How to join
SineSci recruits at the start of each school year, and the application has two phases: an initial application form, then an essay. The forms and the recruitment guidelines are posted on its Facebook page. In 2025 the initial application closed on August 13. Students who love taking photos, shooting video or editing are who the team looks for.

## When it is active
All year, at every big school event. Recruitment runs around August.
