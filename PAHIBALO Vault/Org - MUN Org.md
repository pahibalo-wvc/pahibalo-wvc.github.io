# MUN Org

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The campus Model United Nations group.

## What it is
PisayMUN-WVC (Pisay Model United Nations - WVC on Facebook) is the school's Model United Nations group. It sends delegations to MUN conferences.

## What members do
Delegates research their assigned country, practice public speaking and negotiation, then debate in committees, defend their country's position and draft resolutions. At the 8th San Agustin Model United Nations (SAMUN), October 14 to 16, 2025, the delegation won Best Delegation for the second year in a row. See [[Comp - Model United Nations]].

## When it is active
