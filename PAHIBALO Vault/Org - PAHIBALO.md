## What it is
PAHIBALO is the student org that runs this website. The name is Hiligaynon for "to inform". It gives juniors and incoming scholars a quick way to get tried and tested advice from seniors about life at Pisay.

## What members do
Most of the work goes into written guides on dorm life, academics and extracurriculars, all written by seniors. This website is where those guides live. The org also runs a social media page for updates and answers, and questions come in through a Google Form.

## Committees
Three committees write the guides. Dorm and Livelihood covers dorm etiquette, budgeting, safety and shared living. Academics covers study tips, grading and time management, while Extracurriculars covers orgs, events, competitions and how to file forms like the APF.

The Social Media committee runs the page and answers questions. Creatives makes the posters, infographics and guide layouts. Logistics and Technicals handles the equipment and venue for the culminating activity, along with the question form. Documentation and Publicity proofreads the guides, writes captions and keeps records.
