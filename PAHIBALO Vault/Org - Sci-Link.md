## What it is
Sci-Link is the official English publication of PSHS-WVC, a student broadsheet. The 2018 handbook also listed it as an ALA. Its old website (scilink.pshswvc.net) no longer works.

## What members do
Members write for the paper and take photos, including sports coverage like the football games shown here, and post on the Sci-Link Facebook page. They also compete in the [[Comp - Schools Press Conference]]. Sci-Link posted the 2026 Division Schools Press Conference winners, where the school placed first as Top Performing School.

## How to join
Sci-Link gives out its membership application form at the start of the school year, together with a primer. You choose the roles you want from two groups:
- Content: News, Feature, Science and Technology (S&T), Sports, Opinion and Literature.
- Creatives: Editorial Cartoonist, Layout and Digital Artist, and Photojournalist.

You can pick roles from both groups, but at most three roles in all. Joining Sci-Link is also one way onto the press conference team ([[Comp - Humanities]]).

## When it is active
During the school year, with the press conference season (division round, then regional round). In SY 2026-2027, Sci-Link and Banaag trained together for the Division Schools Press Conference, held on November 7 to 8, 2026.
