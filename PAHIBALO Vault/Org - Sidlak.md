# Sidlak

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> PSHS-WVC's e-gaming org. It runs gaming events and competitions and teaches responsible gaming.

## What it is
Sidlak is the org for students who play e-games, whether competitively or just for fun. It runs gaming events and competitions, along with sessions on responsible gaming. The org wants gaming at school to stay healthy and open to everyone.

Sidlak is a different org from [[Org - Siklab]], the science club.

Its stated mission is to give students a place to explore, enjoy and get good at e-games. Its vision is teamwork and growth in e-gaming within the PSHS-WVC community.

## What members do
See the committees below.

## Committees
Sidlak has three committees. The Creatives committee makes posters, banners and graphics for events.

The Publications committee promotes events online. It also takes photos and videos during events and turns them into photo compilations and gameplay clips. The Finance committee handles the budget, records, fundraising and sponsors.

The officers are an overall head, a head each for internal and external affairs, a secretary, and heads for finance, creatives and publications.

## How to join
Sidlak posts an application form at the start of the school year; the SY 2026-2027 form and a primer were posted on its Facebook page. You can apply to a committee (Creatives, Publications or Finance) or join as a casual member who comes to the events. The membership fee is ₱100, paid when you join, and members are expected to attend events and meetings and help their committee. The org also has a Discord server, with the invite on its Facebook page.
