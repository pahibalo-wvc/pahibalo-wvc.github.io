# Siklab

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> PSHS-WVC's science club. Its Facebook page calls it the school's premiere science club.

## What it is
Siklab is the science club of PSHS-WVC. Its Facebook page calls it the school's premier science club. It is a different org from [[Org - Sidlak]], the e-gaming org.

## What members do
Members plan and run science events. On December 15, 2025, Siklab held HuSciYa 2025 (from "husay", "saya" and "science"), a day of activities and competitions that prepares younger students for high school work. Teams came from schools around the region, including an elementary school, and Grade 11 and 12 LEAP scholars helped with pubmats, decorations and facilitating. The club has an executive council and teacher advisers.

## How to join
Siklab opens a membership application form at the start of each school year and posts the link on its Facebook page. For SY 2026-2027 the deadline was September 7, 2026, at 11:59 PM.

## When it is active
Applications close in early September. HuSciYa ran in December in 2025.
