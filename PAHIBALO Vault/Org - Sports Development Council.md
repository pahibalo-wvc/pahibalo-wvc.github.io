# Sports Development Council

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The student org that runs the school's sports events: Fun Day, Afterburn, Intramurals, the Fun Run and YUPISAY.

## What it is
The Sports Development Council (SDC) is the student org that plans the school's sports events, so scholars stay active and batches get to work together.

## What members do
The SDC has five events lined up for SY 2026-2027.

- Fun Day opens the school year with games around the grounds, oval and quadrangle. In 2026 it was on July 24.
- Afterburn is a break after each quarter exam, run with the [[Org - Student Alliance]]. The 2026 edition had chess, 3x3 basketball, volleyball, and Mobile Legends and Valorant tournaments.
- Intramurals is the school-wide sports fest, played by batch.
- The Fun Run happens during the Foundation Day celebration, and scholars, staff and parents can all join.
- YUPISAY is a friendly sports meet between UP High School and PSHS-WVC.

## Contact
The SDC posts on its Facebook page, "Pisay WVC Sports Development Council".
