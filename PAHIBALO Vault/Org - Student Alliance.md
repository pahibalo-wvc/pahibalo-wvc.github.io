# Student Alliance

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> The student government of PSHS-WVC.

## What it is
The Student Alliance (SA) is the student government of PSHS-WVC, a regular student organization in the handbook. Its office is room AB2 111, at the end of the same hall as the [[Office - Student Affairs Unit]].

## What members do
The SA leads the first turn of flag ceremony hosting and runs Afterburn, the after-exam break event, with the [[Org - Sports Development Council]]. In 2026 it ran Faculty and Staff Day, with student performances for the teachers, and posted the contest rules and program for the Christmas Lantern Lighting ([[Event - Christmas Lighting]]). The 2018 handbook says it donates unclaimed lost-and-found items to charity.

## Consortiums
Consortiums are student groups that help the SA by preparing its pubmats and other materials. How they are formed and how to join: (unknown).

## How to join
Officers are elected every April in the SA elections run by [[Org - COMELEC]]. To run, file a certificate of candidacy when COMELEC opens filing in March (2026: March 24 to 31). Candidates run in parties, campaign for about three weeks and face the school at the Grand Rally before election day. From 2026, batch representatives are elected in the same election. How to join a consortium: (unknown).

## When it is active
The whole school year: flag ceremony hosting, Afterburn after each quarter exam, Faculty and Staff Day, and the Christmas Lantern Lighting. In SY 2026-2027 the SA also held IGP selling days (September 11, and during Afterburn on September 23) and runs a water refill station project for the school year.
