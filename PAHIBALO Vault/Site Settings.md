# Site Settings

Back to [[Home]]

The words and links on the home page, header and footer. Change a value above, run the build, and the site updates. No code needed.

## What each field does
- `hero_note`: the handwritten line above the greeting. Keep it short (2 to 4 words).
- `hero_title`: the big greeting.
- `hero_text`: one sentence under the greeting.
- `search_placeholder`: the grey example text inside the search box.
- `photo_caption`: the handwritten caption under the campus photo.
- `menu_top_count`: how many tabs the top menu shows before the More tab (the rest are in More).
- `menu_pinned`: tabs set apart at the right end of the top menu, just before More (GWA Calc since 2026-11-24).
- `quick_links`: the small buttons under the search box. Each is a link to a note. The part after `|` is the button text.
- `tiles`: the big folder buttons. Use a hub note or the GWA tool.
- `need_text`: the line under "I need to…". The tasks themselves come from [[I Need To]].
- `new_title`: heading of the Starter Kit list on the home page.
- `about_title`, `about_text`: the "About" box near the bottom of the home page.
- `footer_note`: the disclaimer in the footer (D24).
- `site_url`: the live address, used for the sitemap.
- `search_engines`: "off" asks Google and other search engines not to list the site (every page gets a `noindex` tag). Set it to "on" on launch day; the site then becomes searchable and gets a sitemap.
- `report_link`, `ask_link`: Google Form links for "Report an error" and "Ask a question". Leave "(unknown)" until the forms exist.

Writing rule: plain student voice, concrete facts, no invented details. See the writing rules in [[How to Maintain PAHIBALO]].
