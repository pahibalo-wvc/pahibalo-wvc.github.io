## Core values
Pursuit of Truth (integrity, respect), Passion for Excellence (scientific mind, creativity), Commitment to Service (responsibility, commitment).

## Attendance
- Absent all day: missed all classes, or missed a school-organized activity.
- Absenteeism: absent at least 4 days in a quarter.
- Excused: official school representation, sickness, death of immediate family, or similar. Other reasons need advance approval.
- **Unexcused if no excuse slip from the Registrar within 2 days of returning.**
- 3 unexcused absences in a quarter: intervention. 5 tardies: intervention. 5 cuts: intervention.

## Offense levels
- **Level I** (disruptive, no injury): absenteeism, cutting, tardiness, littering, leaving school without permission, gadget use in class, dress code, etc. Interventions: warning, conferences, letter to parents. 6th Level I = Level II.
- **Level II**: academic dishonesty, bullying, fighting, alcohol, tobacco, weapons, ID falsification, etc. Sanctions by Discipline Committee: written reprimand, behavior contract, restitution or reparation. "Needs Improvement" character mark. 6th Level II = Level III.
- **Level III**: theft, assault, drugs, document falsification, etc. Needs Management Committee approval. Sanctions include suspension of privileges (including **leave pass for dormers**) and suspension up to 10 school days. One count = probation next year; two in a year = termination ground.
- **Level IV**: wilful crime endangering life or major property damage. BOT approval. Termination, expulsion or non-graduation.

## Procedure (in brief)
- Anyone may file an **anecdotal report** (signed) with the Discipline Office within **3 school days**.
- Discipline Officer evaluates; Level II+ goes to preliminary inquiry (parents notified in writing; inquiry within 10 school days).
- Formal inquiry by Discipline Committee if needed; a Student Body representative observes without a vote.
- Appeal: written notice within 5 days, up the chain DO, DC, ManCom, BOT.

## Terms
- "Infraction report" in the masterplan = the **Anecdotal Report (AR)** in official wording. Add alias.
