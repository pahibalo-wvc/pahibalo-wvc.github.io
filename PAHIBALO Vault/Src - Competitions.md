## School rules for any competition (solid, 2018 handbook)
- Needs Campus Director approval, requested by the coach.
- Eligibility to miss classes: GWA not below 2.25; no 2.75/3.00/4.00/5.00 last quarter; not on probation. A 2.50 needs an appeal letter and a conference.
- **Student Representation Form** 3 school days before; **admission slip** from the Registrar after; make up work within 5 school days.
- On probation: cannot represent the school.

## PMO: Philippine Mathematical Olympiad
- Run by the Mathematical Society of the Philippines with DOST-SEI (search results, 26th PMO news). Confidence: likely.
- Three stages: qualifying (written), area, national (top 20 of area stage). Older brochures say open to all high school students. Confidence: likely.
- 28th PMO dates seen on pmo.ph snippet: qualifying 18 Oct 2025, area 13 Dec 2025. Time-sensitive; next edition (unknown).
- Official name differs slightly from PAHIBALO team's "Philippine Math Olympiad": use **Philippine Mathematical Olympiad**, alias "Philippine Math Olympiad".

## PBO: Philippine Biology Olympiad
- Hosted by the Department of Biology, Ateneo de Manila University; selects the four-person Philippine team for the International Biology Olympiad (IBO). Confidence: likely (search snippets of ibo-info.org and DepEd memos).
- For Grades 7-12 per one source. Confidence: uncertain.
