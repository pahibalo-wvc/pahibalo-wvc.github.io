## Framework (handbook p. 12)
- Grades 7-8 **Foundation**, 9-10 **Advancement**, 11-12 **Specialization**.
- ALA runs Grades 7-10. **SCALE = Service, Creativity, Action, Leadership Enhancement**, Grades 11-12 (confirmed in the figure). PAHIBALO team: now called LEAP.

## Units rule
6 meetings/week = 2 units; 5 = 1.7; 4 = 1.3; 3 = 1; 2 = 0.7.

## Science Immersion / Internship Program (SIP), p. 23
- Required, non-graded part of the curriculum. Minimum **80 hours**, done in a summer after Grade 9 (proposal: offered to incoming G10 or G11). Lab work, research, design, field work or programming at a partner institution; may be abroad.
- Formerly "Summer Science Internship Program (SSIP)". Name conflict: heading says "Science Immersion Program", body says "Science Internship Program". Both kept as aliases.

## Grading (p. 24)
- Cumulative grading. From Q2 on: **Present quarter grade = (Tentative Grade × 2 + Previous Grade) ÷ 3.**
