# Src - Discipline Office Site

Back to [[Home]]

**Coverage.** DOme, DOssier and DO forms pages read in full. "Copy of DO forms" page not read (looks like a draft duplicate).

## DOme
- DO gives interventions and disciplinary actions; due process.
- **Official lost and found depository.** Use the Lost and Found Item Report form. Unclaimed items after the last day of classes are donated through the Student Alliance.
- Discipline Officers per grade band (G7-8, G9-10, G11-12). Names not recorded here (no personal names on the site).
- Email: disciplineoffice@wvc.pshs.edu.ph. Address: Brgy. Bito-on, Jaro, Iloilo City 5000.

## DO forms (filing)
- Online: pick the form, fill the Google Form, get a PDF confirmation by email (also sent to DO). For AR, uploaded attachments are visible only to DO.
- Traditional: copies of "the three forms" at the DO, or download and print; sign; submit on the shelf.
- Online links found: **AnecDOtal Report Form** (https://forms.gle/xqoSWqJWREwzEb2D9) and **Request DOcuments** form (Google Form).
- Third form is likely the Lost and Found Item Report; its link was not found as text. (unknown)
- AR rules match the Code of Conduct (within 3 school days, signed).
