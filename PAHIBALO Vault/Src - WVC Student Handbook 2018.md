## History and identity
- PSHS-WVC is at Brgy. Bito-on, Jaro, Iloilo City. Lot donated 1991 (3.4 ha), called the Doña Lawa-an H. Lopez Campus.
- Operations began 1 July 1993 with 47 pioneer scholars, classes first held at UP Visayas. Moved to Bito-on in SY 1994-95. Inaugurated 13 June 1997.
- PSHS System Law: RA 8496 (1997), amended by RA 9036 (2001). Under DOST.
- Logo meaning: flame = enthusiasm, ardour, light, warmth; four circles = DOST symbol, "unit particles".
- Core values: Truth, Excellence, Service to nation.

## Offices (org chart text, 2018)
Divisions: Curriculum and Instruction Division (CID); Student Services Division (SSD); Finance and Administration Division (FAD).
Units named: Academic Unit, Student Affairs Unit (SAU), Registrar Unit, Guidance Unit, Student Discipline Office, Health Services Unit, Library Unit, Residence Halls Unit, Cash Management, HR Management, General Services, Records, Accounting, Budget, Supply and Property, Procurement, IT Unit, Planning Office, QMS Office.
- **Resolves:** "SSD" = Student Services Division. "FAD" = Finance and Administration Division (so the "FAD form" likely belongs to this division; exact form name (unknown)).

## Scholarship
- Free tuition, monthly stipend, textbook loan for all. Monthly living allowance for those who qualify by socio-economic status.
- Categories: Full, Partial 1, Partial 2, Partial 3 (set by Scholarship Categorization Committee).
- Grant lasts six years. Must take an S&T course after graduation (list per BOT Res. 2018-06-49) or reimburse.

## Standing, probation, termination
- Good standing: final GWA 2.25 or better; no 5.00 in the final quarter; 3 or fewer unexcused absences.
- Probation: GWA below 2.25, or a Level III+ offense, or 8+ unexcused absences. On probation: no org officer post, no competing for the school.
- Termination (G7-11): a 5.00 in the final quarter, or 13+ unexcused absences, or two Level III+ offenses in a year, or two successive probations.
- Director's List: GWA 1.00 to 1.50.
- Graduation honors: With Highest Honors 1.00-1.20; With High Honors 1.21-1.50; need all final grades 2.50 or better and 4+ years residency. GWA truncated after two decimals.

## Attendance
- Tardy: not in line at flag ceremony start, or 10+ minutes late to class.
- Cutting: on campus but not in class, or left and not back in 10 minutes.
- After tardiness, absence or cutting: get a **Class Admission Slip from the Registrar** before re-entering class.
- Flag ceremony Mondays 7:30-8:00 AM at the gym; flag lowering Fridays 4:00 PM at the flag pole.
- 5 tardies = 1 unexcused absence; 2 cuts = 1 (awards counting).

## ID and uniform
- No ID, no entry. Lost ID: request slip at Registrar, pay at cashier, replacement after 3 working days.
- Boys: white polo with PSHS patch, black pants, black leather shoes. Girls: cream blouse with patch, brown-and-white checkered skirt, white socks, black leather shoes.
- Haircut check: first Monday of the month by homeroom adviser.

## Student services
- Registrar: 7:30 AM-4:30 PM, Mon-Fri. Enrolment, IDs, report cards, transcripts, stipend payroll.
- Year-end clearance signatories: Discipline Officer, Science Lab Technician, Computer Lab Technician, Cashier, Supply and Property Officer, Guidance Counselor, Librarian, Residence Halls Head, Physician/Nurse, Homeroom Adviser, ALA/Club Adviser, SCALE Adviser (G11-12), Division Chiefs, Campus Director (G12).
- Guidance: counseling, homeroom guidance, testing, career guidance.
- Library: 7:30 AM-4:30 PM. Up to 4 books (2 circulation, 2 fiction); circulation 3 days, fiction 1 week. Fines P3/day and P1/day (2018).
- Clinic (Health Services Unit): physician and nurse, 7:30 AM-4:30 PM.

## Residence halls (dorm), 2018
- Two halls, boys and girls. Priority: G7-G8 from the provinces on full scholarship.
- Lodging fee Php 60/month plus electric bill, deducted from stipend (2018).
- Wake-up 5:30-6:00 AM. Out of dorm by 7:00 AM Monday (flag ceremony), 7:30 AM Tue-Fri. Dorm closed 7:30 AM-4:00 PM weekdays.
- Campus curfew 6:00 PM Mon-Fri. Dorm curfew 7:00 PM daily. Study period 8:00-9:00 PM (Sun-Thu).
- Visitors at reception only, must log in. Visiting hours: 7:00 AM-12:00 noon Sat-Sun; 3:00-8:00 PM holidays.
- **Leave pass types:** Regular (short errands, weekends), Home (going home Wednesdays or weekends), Official (school functions; needs parent consent).
- Interns may only leave campus with passes signed by Dorm Managers/SSD or CISD Chiefs.
- A separate **Dormitory Handbook** exists for residents (not found online).
- Laundry: laundry women allowed in at 4:30 PM Mon-Thu, 4:00 PM Fri (security rules). Laundry slip itself: (unknown).

## Competitions and representation
- Representing the school needs Campus Director approval via the coach.
- Eligibility to miss classes for outside events: GWA not below 2.25; no 2.75, 3.00, 4.00 or 5.00 last quarter; not on probation. A 2.50 needs an appeal letter and conference.
- **Student Representation Form (SRF):** fill out 3 school days before the activity; subject teachers list missed work and sign. Marked "On Competition", not absent.
- After returning: **admission slip from the Registrar**; finish missed work within 5 school days.
- Off-campus activities: parent permit and 1 teacher-chaperone per 15 students.

## Orgs and ALAs (2018)
- Regular student orgs: Student Alliance (SA), Sports Development Council (SDC), Homeroom Organization.
- ALAs compulsory for Grades 7-10. 2018 list: Anime Club, Stellar Club, Sports Club, Photography, Volleyball, ICT and Robotics, Martial Arts, Social Transformers, Performing Arts, Public Speaking, League of Science Scholars, Kaibigan (Peer Support Group), 3-D Modelling and Game Development, Student Broadsheets (Banaag and Sci-Link), K-Pop Club, Math ALA. "ALA offerings vary each school year."
- Optional clubs: Bible Study, Youth for Christ, AGHAM.
- Banaag and Sci-Link are the student broadsheets (school papers).
- G7-10 join 1 to 3 orgs/ALAs; G11-12 optional. Org needs 15+ members and SAU accreditation (application letter, constitution, calendar, adviser statement, officers, members). Only students in good standing can be officers.

## Campus rules
- Gates open 6:00 AM-7:00 PM weekdays; closed weekends and no-class days.
- Externs get lockers at the start of the year.
- Lost and found: Discipline Office; owner presents a Lost Item Report.
- Ballgames not allowed 7:30 AM-4:30 PM except PE.
