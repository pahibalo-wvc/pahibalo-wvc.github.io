# GWA Calculator

Back to [[Home]]

> [!summary] In short
> Built from scratch. Runs in the browser; nothing is stored.

## Spec
1. Pick a grade set: G7, G8, G9, G10, G10 without elective, G11, G12. ("SYP" set from the reference site: (unknown).)
2. Enter each subject's grade (1.00 to 5.00).
3. GWA = sum of (grade × units) ÷ sum of units. Show every line.
4. Units come from [[Guide - Curriculum and Units]] (2015 matrix). Mark them "verify" until confirmed current.
5. Disclaimer under the result.
