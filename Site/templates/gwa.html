How this is worked out

GWA = sum of (grade × units) ÷ sum of units of the graded subjects. Values Education is marked Complete or Incomplete, so it is left out. Like the report card, the result is cut to two decimals, not rounded (Grading System). Grades 8 and 9 use units from current report cards; the other grades still use the 2015 curriculum matrix (Curriculum and Units), so check with your adviser.
